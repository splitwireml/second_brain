# Stav Zilbershtein

Stav Zilbershtein (@stavzilber) is an X creator documenting AI-assisted advertising and video-production workflows.

## Source contribution

His July 2026 X article presents a conversational system for producing paper-collage / Vox-style animated ads. The workflow combines a short product interview, product-image references, mascot or faceless modes, chained 10-second clips, and a MaxFusion AI MCP controlled through [[claude]].

The article also argues for using Google Omni Flash rather than Seedance for this format, emphasizing legible on-screen text, clean narration, continuity, render speed, and reroll cost. These comparative claims are recorded as source claims rather than independently verified conclusions.
