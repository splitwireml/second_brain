# Demis Hassabis

Demis Hassabis is the named author of the local X article **A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age**. In that article, he argues that AGI may be only a few years away and proposes a U.S.-initiated [[frontier-ai-standards-body]] for testing and governing frontier models. These are positions and forecasts reported by the source, not independently verified facts. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-a-framework-for-frontier-ai-and-the-dawning-of-a-n-2076957440109625718.md]

## Position in this source

- Frames AGI as a potentially transformative technology whose benefits depend on responsible deployment.
- Calls for cautious optimism, public policy that balances innovation with responsibility and security, and international collaboration.
- Connects control of increasingly agentic, recursively self-improving systems to rigorous evaluation and human judgment, making the proposal adjacent to [[human-in-the-loop]] practice.
