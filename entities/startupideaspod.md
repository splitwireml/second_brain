# The Startup Ideas Podcast

The Startup Ideas Podcast (SIP, @startupideaspod) is an AI/startup-focused podcast and X publisher that turns builder/operator interviews into practical playbooks for product development, AI tools, marketing, and AI-native business models.

## Source Notes

- [Seedance 2.0: The New Default Video Model](https://x.com/startupideaspod/status/2045221480120885529) — Interview with Sirio walking through Seedance 2.0's multi-input video generation workflow, prompting techniques, and production use cases.
- [Agents are the new SaaS](https://x.com/startupideaspod/status/2072451543073439905) — Playbook for selling agent products as labor replacement rather than software seats: pick paid workflows, shadow the human operator, build bounded autonomy, wrap the agent with logs/approvals/evals, sell pilots, then productize repeatable delivery.

## Themes

### AI media as operator playbook

SIP's useful pattern is not just reporting that a tool or model exists. Its X articles package AI shifts as operator checklists: Seedance 2.0 becomes a directing workflow for AI video, while agent SaaS becomes a step-by-step business model for replacing narrowly scoped human labor with AI systems.

### From tools to outcomes

The agent SaaS article fits the broader [[services-as-software]] / [[ai-native-services-agency]] cluster: the buyer is not paying for access to a dashboard, but for a job that no longer needs to be handled manually. SIP's framing adds a product-building discipline around workflow selection, human shadowing, eval sets, and trust wrappers.
