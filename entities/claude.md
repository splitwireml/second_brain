## Overview

**Claude** is Anthropic's family of large language models. Used as research tool for technical analysis and wiki authoring. See [[anthropic]] for the company.

## Model Variants

| Variant | Positioning | Key Trait |
|---|---|---|
| Haiku | Fast, cheap | Speed over depth |
| Sonnet | Balanced | General-purpose workhorse |
| Opus | Deep reasoning | Complex engineering, long-context |
| Mythos | Research preview | Found 27-year-old OpenBSD vulnerability by reading full source tree |

## Recent Capabilities (2026)

- **End-to-end outcome ownership** — applies judgment to complete high-ambiguity tasks (Opus 4.7, Mythos Preview) ^[raw/articles/jouhatsu-code-with-claude-london-2026-05-21.md]
- **Computer use** — safe GUI automation (since Sonnet 3.5)
- **Thinking** — adapts reasoning depth to task (since Sonnet 3.7)
- **Agentic planning** — holds plans over hundreds of steps ^[raw/articles/jouhatsu-code-with-claude-london-2026-05-21.md]
- **Fable 5 advertorial production** — Amin's source-described workflow uses Claude Fable 5 for awareness-matched Google Ads advertorials, constrained prompt blocks, and revision audits. The reported time and ROAS gains are source claims, not verified benchmarks. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-cook-killer-google-ads-advertorials-with-fab-2076724912937750754.md]
