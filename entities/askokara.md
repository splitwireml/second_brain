# askOkara

Startup founder (@askOkara) who built a tool that analyzes your product site, defines your ICP, and deploys AI agents to drive traffic and acquire users.

## Overview

Shared a thread promoting their product for getting first 100 users by dropping a product URL — the system analyzes the site, defines ICP, and deploys AI agents. The thread generated significant feedback, both positive (impressed users, bug reports) and negative (support issues, handle bans).

## Key Claims

- System deploys specialized AI agents that work 24/7 to drive traffic and users
- Multiple users reported bugs: handle bans, blank analysis pages, login/session issues
- Creator responded publicly acknowledging issues and deploying fixes
- A July 2026 article by [[fatimarizwan]] uses Okara as a launch case study: visitors type a website, log in, and see agents start working before being asked for additional onboarding steps; this is a source-described product flow.
- The article says the team eventually automated its manually run influencer-recruitment process into Okara's influencer agent after negotiating rates, coordinating launch-day posts, and clearing payments individually; these are source-reported operational details.
