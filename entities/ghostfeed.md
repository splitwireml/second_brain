# Ghostfeed

Ghostfeed is an AI UGC production and scheduling product named in [[type-kshitij]]'s guide as a no-code route for generating TikTok slideshows and setting an automated monthly posting schedule.

## Positioning

The article describes Ghostfeed as a way to create unlimited slideshows and automate a posting calendar, with Reel Farm presented as an alternative. These are source-described product claims. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-no-bs-guide-to-ai-ugc-at-scale-2049286061105483868.md]

A current canonical-site check on 2026-08-04 corroborated the product category: [ghostfeed.ai](https://ghostfeed.ai/) describes “AI TikTok Video Cloner, UGC Reactions, and Slideshows,” with the headline “Operating System for running AI UGC at Scale.” This confirms positioning, not the article's generation costs, volume, views, or conversion results.

## Workflow role

Ghostfeed sits at the generation/scheduling layer of [[ai-ugc-persona-factory]]: the operator supplies a locked persona and format direction, then uses the service to produce or schedule recurring output. The broader category is [[ai-ugc]]; distribution still depends on the target platform and account infrastructure.
