# Lumen

Lumen (@lumenxbt) authored the local X thread **Ten million people have watched an MIT professor accidentally destroy the executive coaching industry**. The thread presents Patrick Winston's “How to Speak” lecture as a compact public-speaking framework built around visual presence, simplicity, empathy, and a memorable close. This is a source-described synthesis; the thread's external claims and metrics were not independently verified. ^[raw/articles/thread-lumenxbt-2083603666603528596.md]

## Source contribution

- Names Patrick Winston, the MIT Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, and the annual “How to Speak” lecture as the source of the framework.
- Preserves the napkin-sized rule set: “Do not read. Be in the image. Keep images simple. Eliminate clutter. Start with an empathetic connection. End with a punch line the audience can repeat over dinner. Never open with a joke. Never end with \"thank you.\"” ^[raw/articles/thread-lumenxbt-2083603666603528596.md]
- Says the lecture is freely available through MIT OpenCourseWare and that Winston's textbook is free on his page, but the local source provides no destination URLs and no external page was fetched. ^[raw/articles/thread-lumenxbt-2083603666603528596.md]
