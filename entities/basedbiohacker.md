# BasedBiohacker

BasedBiohacker is an X creator focused on self-experimentation, performance, longevity, and business self-direction. In this source he frames wealth-building as a process of matching a business vehicle to temperament, skills, dislikes, risk tolerance, and execution capacity rather than copying whatever model is currently fashionable.

## What this source adds

- Argues that there is no universal "best" business model; fit is individual and depends on traits, preferences, and constraints.
- Uses process-of-elimination to rule out misaligned paths such as sales, finance, trading, and agency work for someone who dislikes social pressure, numerical work, or client dependence.
- Recommends working backward from a concrete income target into offer size, client count, channel economics, and activity volume.
- Emphasizes execution over prolonged research: roughly 10 hours doing for every 1 hour planning after the initial decision window.
