# Distribb

Distribb is an SEO and backlinks automation product associated with [[borja]] / Bomx. In the July 2026 X article, it is positioned as an automation layer for an AI visibility audit: teams can map where AI systems recommend competitors, identify the sources those systems cite, and package the workflow as a Claude/Cursor/Codex-compatible skill. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-2026-ai-visibility-audit-do-or-die-2074830918167015884.md]

## Operating pattern

The product pitch turns [[llm-seo]] from a vague "AI search strategy" into a repeatable audit: run the same high-intent buyer queries across multiple AI systems, track recommendations and cited sources, rank the reachable gaps, and then improve owned pages with clearer answers, formatting, and proof. This connects Distribb to the wiki's existing SEO automation cluster, including [[Bomx-qwoted-seo-backlinks-skill]], [[claude]], [[cursor]], and [[codex]]. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-2026-ai-visibility-audit-do-or-die-2074830918167015884.md]

## Broken-link outreach feature

Borja's July 2026 follow-up describes a Distribb Link Outreach feature that finds broken pages with backlinks, reads the original resource's link intent, creates and publishes a replacement asset, verifies the right contact, drafts personalized outreach for approval, and tracks replies and follow-ups. The source presents this as the productized form of [[broken-link-building]], with [[kimi-k3]] doing the heavy hunting while a stronger model retains judgment. These capabilities and the article's performance/cost claims are source-described, not independently audited. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-kimi-k3-my-new-head-of-seo-link-outreach-broken-li-2079207506581045451.md]
