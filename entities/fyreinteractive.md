# Haris (@fyreinteractive)

Haris is the author of a substantive local X Article describing a source-claimed YouTube analytics workflow that connects Nexlev MCP's 60+ natural-language research tools to Opus 5 inside a Claude Project. The article frames the combination as a replacement for a human channel analyst, but its model, tool, pricing, performance, and business results are source-described rather than independently verified. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-turn-nexlev-mcp-opus-5-into-a-247-youtube-v-2081430939213906262.md]

## Source-specific system

The article supplies a `channel-analyst.md` Markdown skill file for project knowledge. It encodes four modes—full channel audit, competitor benchmarking, niche opportunity scan, and retention diagnosis—around retention, script structure, outlier analysis, and competitive positioning. The proposed analyst pulls the last 30 videos for an audit, 20 videos per competitor for benchmarking, transcript evidence for retention diagnosis, and Nexlev data for exact-number recommendations. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-turn-nexlev-mcp-opus-5-into-a-247-youtube-v-2081430939213906262.md]

The same source describes a FacelessOS loop in which research identifies an opportunity, analytics diagnoses the gap, a script brief encodes the fix, and skill files produce the next script batch. It attributes 200+ creators, 15 additional skill files, 6 power workflows, and calibration across 7,000+ scripts and 42+ niches to that system; later copy in the source states 8,000+ scripts, $5M+ generated for clients, 21 Claude skill files, and 50+ niches. These are source claims, not audited operating metrics. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-turn-nexlev-mcp-opus-5-into-a-247-youtube-v-2081430939213906262.md]
