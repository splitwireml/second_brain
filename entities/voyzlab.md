# Voyz (VoyzLab)

Voyz (`@voyzlab`) is the author of the source-described **AI Video Workflow 2026** guide. The article frames cinematic AI video as a directed, shot-based production process rather than a sequence of isolated prompts.

## Source-specific contribution

The guide's central rule is to plan the film before opening a generator: define the emotional core, choose a beat structure, create a 5–8 shot list with framing and camera movement, lock characters through reference images, match models to shot requirements, generate sound-critical shots early, and unify the final cut with editing and color grade. The reusable framework is filed as [[ai-video-director-workflow]].

The article is also a practical comparison of current tool roles: Claude or ChatGPT for structure, Google Flow for storyboard-to-video work, Midjourney Omni Reference (`--oref`, `--ow`) and Nano Banana Pro for identity-consistent stills, Higgsfield for multi-model generation, Seedance 2.0/Veo 3.1/Kling 3.0 for shot-specific video, ElevenLabs for voice and sound effects, and CapCut/Runway/Descript for editing.
