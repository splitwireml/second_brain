# Imran

Imran ([@lmrankhan](https://x.com/lmrankhan)) is an X creator writing about startup formation, market timing, product wedges, and founder execution. In this source he frames company-building less as finding an untouched market and more as spotting a secular shift early, winning power users first, and persisting before the opportunity looks obvious to everyone else.

## What this source adds

- Emphasizes **non-consensus beliefs** as the starting point for category-defining startups.
- Uses [[cursor]], [[figma]], and [[shopify]] as examples of companies that looked early or non-obvious when they began.
- Treats **distribution** and customer learning as first-order founder work rather than post-build marketing.
- Argues that persistence matters because the market often validates the idea long after the founder starts building.
