# DegenCalls

DegenCalls (`@Degen_calls_sol`) is an X creator writing about AI-assisted [[knowledge-management]], [[obsidian]], and second-brain workflows. The ingested article argues that the practical breakthrough is not another note-taking interface, but delegating wiki maintenance to LLMs so raw sources, synthesis pages, schemas, indexes, and health checks compound over time.

## Relevant Source

- **Your Second Brain Is Useless Until AI Maintains It** — frames [[andrej-karpathy]]'s raw-source-plus-wiki pattern as a shift from one-off retrieval to a maintained markdown knowledge base where the human curates inputs and the AI handles repetitive structure.^[raw/articles/xarticle-your-second-brain-is-useless-until-ai-maintains-it-2073375316840415716.md]
