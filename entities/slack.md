## Overview

**Slack** is a business communication platform. Known for its warm empty state messaging that sets an emotional tone for new users.

## Key Facts

- Founded: 2009
- Headquarters: San Francisco, CA
- Type: SaaS communication platform
- Acquired by Salesforce in 2021

## Empty State Design

Slack's empty channel says something warm — setting an emotional tone that makes users feel welcome rather than confused.
