# PM OS

PM OS, the Product Manager's AI Operating System, is a source-described collection of 200+ skills for product-management work. The local article says it runs in [[claude-code]], Claude Cowork, or Cursor over a company's context files so outputs such as success-metric definitions and review plans are grounded in product-specific constraints rather than generic advice. These details are recorded as claims from this source, not independently verified product documentation.^[raw/articles/xarticle-success-metric-never-ever-worry-again-about-settin-2075879695841968335.md]

## Role in the source

The article uses `success-metric` as one example skill inside PM OS. It places that skill beside broader workflows for strategy, research, decisions, stakeholder work, and measurement, framing PM OS as an operating layer rather than a standalone metric template.^[raw/articles/xarticle-success-metric-never-ever-worry-again-about-settin-2075879695841968335.md]
