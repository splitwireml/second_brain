# ElevenLabs

AI voice synthesis and voice cloning platform. Leading TTS provider with natural prosody.

## Source-described narration role

Sleep's local X Article names ElevenLabs for AI narration across the hook variants at a source-stated $22 monthly cost within a $264 four-platform stack. It provides no voice model/version, prompt, API/interface, audio format, file path, or handoff specification; the role and cost remain source-described. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-weve-generated-5b-views-heres-how-to-go-viral-ever-2082122676580098492.md]

## Cinematic post-production role

The [[voyzlab]] workflow names ElevenLabs for cloned or synthetic dialogue, text-described sound effects, video-to-sound suggestions, and dubbing. It places audio before the final edit rather than treating sound as an afterthought, but supplies no independent capability test, model/version, or API handoff. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-ai-video-workflow-2026-cinematic-masterpiece-2078133327714738454.md]

## Faceless YouTube narration role

Miss Scarlett's source places ElevenLabs after Claude's script and before visual generation: paste the script into the voice tool, generate the narration, download it, and use its pauses to place one new visual per beat. The article recommends keeping one voice for channel identity and describes the cost as approximately `$22` per month. It gives no voice model/version, prompt, API/interface, audio format, file path, or export setting; the role and price are source-described. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-built-faceless-youtube-channel-with-claude-today-2084611385376285065.md]
