# Will Chen

Will Chen (@stablechen) wrote the local X Article **A beginner's guide to metacognition**. The article treats metacognition as computational debugging for the human half of a human–AI system, rather than as generic journaling or meditation. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-a-beginners-guide-to-metacognition-2079624266707054825.md]

## Source contribution

- Frames working memory, energy, beliefs, context loading, and attention as bounded system resources shared by human and AI work.
- Offers four diagnostic readings for friction: operating level, divergence versus convergence, contact with reality, and remaining budget.
- Recommends turning labels such as lazy or undisciplined into mechanisms that can be inspected and changed.
