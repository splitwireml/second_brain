# vivek

X writer focused on research practice, intellectual training, and how to develop better judgment in machine-learning-adjacent work.

## Known Themes
- Research is a trainable stack of subskills rather than a mystical talent
- Strong researchers choose problems deliberately instead of inheriting them from the current hype cycle
- Writing, prediction, and fast experimental loops are core research infrastructure
- Public explanation is both a contribution and a credibility signal

## Why This Matters

In his essay on [[research-skill-building]], he frames research quality as compounding habits: choosing live problems, upgrading inputs, logging belief updates, tightening experimental loops, and building an open network of collaborators.
