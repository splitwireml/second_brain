# Eden

Eden is a software and creator-workflow platform described by [[dan-koe]] in the captured X Article. This page records the source's product description and promotional framing; availability, implementation, performance, and pricing claims were not independently verified. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-remember-everything-you-read-stop-trying-2081415714636996844.md]

## Source-described capabilities

- **Library, Reader, and Highlights** — a connected library for posts, links, and ideas; save Substack articles, X articles, or YouTube videos, read their transcripts, and highlight them so ideas remain available for later creative work. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-remember-everything-you-read-stop-trying-2081415714636996844.md]
- **Creator research and publishing** — research creators' top content, find a next viral topic, and schedule posts for publication; the article describes an outlier-search feed for finding high-performing social posts in a niche and saving them directly to the library. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-remember-everything-you-read-stop-trying-2081415714636996844.md]
- **Capture and enrichment** — auto-tag, categorize, and embed everything added to the library in a claimed "meaning space"; Eden can also integrate with Kindle to pull in prior highlights. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-remember-everything-you-read-stop-trying-2081415714636996844.md]
- **Meaning-based retrieval** — the article says each idea is represented by approximately `~1500` numbers that act as GPS coordinates, allowing retrieval even when ideas share zero words. It contrasts this with keyword search and says the system can surface novel connections without repeatedly re-searching the whole knowledge base. These are source-described implementation claims. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-remember-everything-you-read-stop-trying-2081415714636996844.md]
- **AI access** — Eden is described as having an MCP integration for building and accessing the knowledge base with Claude, while avoiding the user's need to maintain a vector database, embedding API, indexing scripts, re-embedding after note changes, rename/delete synchronization, or unchanged-content caching. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-remember-everything-you-read-stop-trying-2081415714636996844.md]

## Role in the two-option workflow

The source contrasts **Option 1**, a self-built [[claude-obsidian-second-brain-stack]] using Claude Code and [[obsidian]], with **Option 2**, a managed library such as MyMind or Eden that performs automatic organization and semantic retrieval. MyMind is mentioned as an alternative, but the source explicitly leaves some of its behavior uncertain; no separate MyMind page is inferred. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-remember-everything-you-read-stop-trying-2081415714636996844.md]

## Evidence boundary

The local source supports the named product, its source-described features, the Eden MCP claim, and a promotional offer for four months plus Creator Bootcamp (normally `$499`). It does not independently establish product availability, technical architecture, recommendation quality, semantic-search accuracy, or the reported user praise. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-remember-everything-you-read-stop-trying-2081415714636996844.md]
