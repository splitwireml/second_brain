# Matt Canham

Matt Canham (@matthewcanham) is the author of a local X thread, “Jev Explained for Normies,” which presents TypeSafe AI's [[jev]] as a typed, non-generative decision model for product software. The source contrasts repeatable decision calls with LLM text generation and gives JSON-shaped Noul, Choice, and Score examples.^[raw/articles/thread-matthewcanham-2102077098756280413.md]

## Source contribution

The thread's reusable contribution is a product-builder explanation of [[system-one-models]]: provide `state`, a constrained question/answer contract, and criteria; receive a selected decision and probabilities; keep the product's threshold and follow-up action outside the model. It names `jev-latest`, up to 255 Choice options, and 2–10 Score levels, while treating latency/cost ranges as source claims and leaving model-scale, calibration, and benchmark details unavailable.^[raw/articles/thread-matthewcanham-2102077098756280413.md]
