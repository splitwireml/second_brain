# Miss Scarlett (@vampScally)

Miss Scarlett is the author of a local X Article describing a source-claimed, faceless YouTube production system built around [[claude]], AI narration, image/motion generation, and manual assembly. The article attributes 100,000 subscribers, more than $50,000 in recent YouTube revenue, and roughly one hour per day to the author's operation; those outcomes and the platform-policy, RPM, pricing, and monetization claims remain source-described rather than independently verified.^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-built-faceless-youtube-channel-with-claude-today-2084611385376285065.md]

## Source-specific workflow

The article's sequence is niche selection by RPM and repeatability, weekly title ideation, retention-oriented scripting, voice generation, one visual per narration pause, CapCut assembly with auto-captions, and Canva thumbnail/metadata packaging. It emphasizes keeping a consistent voice, adding original written value and visual variation, batching production, and scheduling posts through the week. The source names Claude, ElevenLabs, Kling, Runway, Higgsfield, CapCut, and Canva but does not specify model versions, APIs, file paths, export settings, or scheduler implementation.^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-built-faceless-youtube-channel-with-claude-today-2084611385376285065.md]
