# CEO Vlad (@CEO_Vlad)

The local X Article metadata identifies the author as “CEO” with handle @CEO_Vlad. The source describes a source-claimed ecommerce advertising workflow built around long-form AI podcast-style ads, Claude-assisted scripts, and high-volume creative testing. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-do-6mmonth-with-my-ecom-brand-using-ai-podca-2080778980555133219.md]

## Source contribution

- Frames two-person podcast ads as native content for TikTok/Reels-style feeds, with a conversational product mention rather than a direct pitch.
- Uses Claude to produce dialogue and 20 hook variations per product, then generates the video in Infinite UGC with a multi-scene Nebula agent, captions, B-roll, and product shots.
- Tests 20+ creative variants in Meta and TikTok Ads Manager, using CTR, hold rate, and ROAS as staged signals and iterating on winning hooks.

The source attributes a $6M/month ecommerce result and specific cost, speed, and performance thresholds to this workflow; those figures are source claims, not independently verified results.
