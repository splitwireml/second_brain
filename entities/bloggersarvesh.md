## March 2026 survival-playbook follow-up

The follow-up X Article reframes the existing system as an eight-audit, 90-day execution order for a cash-constrained local business: categories and attributes first; services and description next; reviews and responses after that; then GBP posts and photos; followed by weekly execution. This is the author's source-described playbook, not an independently validated turnaround plan.

The article also makes a human/automation boundary explicit: Claude gathers competitor and profile data, while the operator chooses revenue-relevant keywords, interprets the local market, and distinguishes SEO signals from offline reputation and relationships.
