# Sarvesh Shrivastava

## Overview

Sarvesh Shrivastava (@bloggersarvesh) is a local SEO practitioner and founder of [[alventra-marketing]]. He runs a marketing agency focused on home services businesses (plumbers, HVAC, contractors, cleaning companies) and has published a detailed 20-prompt [[claude-cowork-seo-system]] that replaces manual SEO audits with AI-augmented workflows.

## March 2026 survival-playbook follow-up

The follow-up X Article reframes the existing system as an eight-audit, 90-day execution order for a cash-constrained local business: categories and attributes first; services and description next; reviews and responses after that; then GBP posts and photos; followed by weekly execution. This is the author's source-described playbook, not an independently validated turnaround plan.

The article also makes a human/automation boundary explicit: Claude gathers competitor and profile data, while the operator chooses revenue-relevant keywords, interprets the local market, and distinguishes SEO signals from offline reputation and relationships.

## Public website prompt-library follow-up

Alventra's public "Top 22 Claude Prompts For SEO" page publishes the fuller prompt mechanics behind the same [[claude-cowork-seo-system]]. It names the required context inputs, competitor GBP files, Chrome/Google Maps handoffs, spreadsheet outputs, and the SEMrush, Google Search Console, Ahrefs, Google Analytics 4, Wikidata, Rich Results Test, and WordPress interfaces used across the audits. ^[raw/articles/alventra-marketing-claude-prompts-2026-07-27.md]

This source introduces version drift: the X Article calls the system a 20-part stack, while the public page labels it 22 prompts and adds a detailed on-page audit plus a backlink-gap audit. The website's later 12-week usage order still enumerates prompts 1–20, so the placement and execution schedule for prompts 21–22 are not specified. The site also repeats service, ranking, revenue, and $1.7M case-study claims; these remain source-reported and unverified. ^[raw/articles/alventra-marketing-claude-prompts-2026-07-27.md]
