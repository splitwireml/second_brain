# Remotion

Remotion is the React-based video-rendering framework used by Matt Chow's team as the code-native composition layer for Trope's launch video. The source describes it as React that renders to video and emphasizes that product scenes were rebuilt as React rather than captured as screen recordings. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-we-made-our-yc-launch-video-in-15-days-with-fa-2075672770483269788.md]

## Source-described workflow role

Fable 5 generated seven product scenes from the team's storyboard and product code, then helped add title overlays, captions, voiceover-synced animation, motion graphics, transitions, pacing, and assembly inside the Remotion project. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-we-made-our-yc-launch-video-in-15-days-with-fa-2075672770483269788.md]
