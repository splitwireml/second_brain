# HardLaunch UGC Agency

[[mattgittleson]] describes HardLaunch UGC Agency as the operation through which he now runs UGC for many brands and niches using the same playbook he first ran for his own app and [[jenni-ai]]. The local article does not establish incorporation, ownership, staffing, pricing, or a separate verified company profile. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

## Source-described delivery system

The four stages are format/distribution-first product selection, founder-run account and warmup before hiring, product-purpose creative with comment conversion work, then additive iteration and cross-creator propagation. Founder-owned taste is separate from delegated creator execution: the author sets formats/hooks/principles, operational staff handle briefing/filming/editing/scheduling/daily management, and he performs the final taste pass on every client campaign (20% / 75% / 5%). Draft review and creator messaging happen every day. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

Its reported campaign evidence includes 1.4B views in 90 days, campaigns below $0.30 CPM, 350,000 extra downloads in four weeks at 7-cent CPI without paid spend, and a 60M-view original followed within 30 days by two more videos above 20M, nine above 5M, and thirty-four above 1M, with follow-ups totaling over 100M. These are source-described outcomes, not independently verified analytics; the article does not identify all clients or map every headline result to one campaign. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

The source says organic attention remains useful months after publication whereas paid attention stops with spend. It estimates equivalent paid reach would cost north of fourteen million dollars, predicts organic CPMs will rise and formats will exhaust faster, and forecasts category ownership for those building the machine in the next twelve months. These comparisons and forecasts are the author's claims, not externally checked rates or predictions. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]
