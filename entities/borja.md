Borja's July 2026 article frames AI visibility as a practical buyer-query audit: ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini purchase-intent questions, record which brands are recommended, identify cited sources such as news sites, Reddit, LinkedIn, and podcasts, then prioritize the realistic source gaps where the brand can appear. He positions this as an extension of [[llm-seo]] rather than a detached replacement for traditional SEO. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-2026-ai-visibility-audit-do-or-die-2074830918167015884.md]

A second July 2026 article turns broken-link building into a repeatable AI workflow: rank competitor 404s by live referring domains, recover the old page's link intent, publish a replacement that matches that intent, and send a precise link-swap pitch.
