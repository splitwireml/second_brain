# Levi Munneke

Levi Munneke is an X creator (@levikmunneke) focused on outbound systems, GTM automation, and AI-assisted lead generation. Across his posts, the recurring thesis is that better prospecting comes from building proprietary data pipelines rather than renting the same saturated contact pools everyone else buys.

## What this source adds

- Extends Levi's prospecting thesis downstream into execution: not just building better lead lists, but running eight-person multichannel outbound through LinkedIn plus email with one shared CRM and strict sender continuity.
- Makes his channel split explicit: [[cold-email]] is the high-volume discovery layer for testing pains, titles, and industries, while LinkedIn is the warmer precision layer used to convert the patterns email uncovers.
- Frames AI as rep memory and reflexes rather than just a copywriter: enrichment before send, draft replies on inbound response, and automated follow-through in [[n8n]] after positive intent appears.
- Extends his earlier Google Maps / Codex framing into a broader public-data thesis: regulatory boards, business registries, nonprofit filings, industry directories, and event lists are all lead sources if you can normalize and enrich them.
- Reframes AI coding tools as scraper-maintenance infrastructure, not just one-off code generators: the durable edge is fast repair when public directory HTML changes.
- Emphasizes list quality over list size by using mandatory-registration datasets and publicly visible business intent signals instead of generic aggregator records.
