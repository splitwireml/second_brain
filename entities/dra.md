# Dra

Dra (@draprints) is an X creator/operator selling cold outbound systems across LinkedIn and email for B2B businesses. The July 2026 source frames recently funded startups as high-intent outbound targets because they have fresh budget, investor pressure to show growth, reachable founders or early executives, and shorter buying paths than enterprise accounts.

## Operating thesis

Dra's useful contribution to the wiki's [[outbound]] cluster is the trigger-based targeting rule: a fresh funding round is not just firmographic data, but a timing signal. A company that raised this month is likely to have more budget and urgency than one that raised years ago, especially when the funding signal is combined with active hiring or other spending evidence.
