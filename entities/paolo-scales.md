# Paolo Trivellato

Paolo Trivellato (@paolo_scales) is an X creator focused on LinkedIn client acquisition systems for agencies, consultants, and B2B service businesses.

## Known For

- [[linkedin-growth]] — argues that most LinkedIn failure comes from treating the platform as a content channel instead of a full funnel

## Core Ideas

- A LinkedIn profile should behave like a landing page, not a résumé.
- Content needs a conversion mechanism: comment CTA, lead magnet, DM sequence, and clear next step.
- DM follow-up should nurture over several touches instead of pitch-slapping on touch one.
