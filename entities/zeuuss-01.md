## Playable web-game brief

The July 27, 2026 local thread asks to build **Sands of the Lost Carpet**, an original game with no existing IP: a fast, stylized third-person / 2.5D endless flying-runner in which the player rides a magic carpet, dodges obstacles, collects gold coins and gems, and escapes a collapsing cavern into a desert oasis. Score is distance + coins; a run ends on 3 hits or a crash. ^[raw/articles/thread-zeuuss_01-2081837342726214087.md]

The requested controls are deliberately simple for desktop + mobile: Left / Right via A-D or arrow keys, or tilt/drag on touch, steer the carpet; Up / Down via W-S or swipe, climb / dive; Space / tap triggers BOOST, a short speed burst limited by an energy meter. ^[raw/articles/thread-zeuuss_01-2081837342726214087.md]

The gameplay brief specifies auto-forward flight at steady speed through falling rocks, stone pillars, and molten bursts inside the cavern; floating gold coins for score; rare blue gems for a bonus + boost refill; a transition after ~30 seconds through a giant beast-mouth cave into a bright oasis; a second obstacle set of palm trees, waterfalls, and city spires; and difficulty that ramps through faster speed and denser obstacles. ^[raw/articles/thread-zeuuss_01-2081837342726214087.md]

The requested visual direction is vibrant stylized-cartoon, “Pixar-meets-Fortnite,” with a saturated gold-teal-magenta palette, warm sunset light, glowing bloom, and particle sparkles. The two environments are a collapsing golden treasure cavern and a lush oasis valley with a turquoise river, waterfalls, and a domed palace-city at sunset. The fully original character is a young acrobat hero in a teal hood and vest, red sash, and tan trousers, riding an ornate patterned carpet with a small original critter companion clinging on; the brief explicitly says no Disney designs. ^[raw/articles/thread-zeuuss_01-2081837342726214087.md]

The HUD must show score top-right, coin counter, a BOOST energy bar, 3 hit-point hearts top-left, and a distance meter. The UI includes a simple start screen with the title + “Tap to Fly,” a game-over screen with score + “Retry” button, rising difficulty, a coin pickup sound, and a boost whoosh. The delivery constraint is to make it smooth, juicy, and instantly playable in the browser, using original assets and characters only—no logos, brand marks, or real game titles. ^[raw/articles/thread-zeuuss_01-2081837342726214087.md]

This source is a design/build brief, not evidence that the game was implemented or tested. It does not name an engine, renderer, framework, command, deployment host, asset-generation tool, browser API, accessibility behavior, performance target, or QA procedure; none is inferred here. ^[raw/articles/thread-zeuuss_01-2081837342726214087.md]
