# Matt (@mattgittleson)

Matt is the exact-handle author of the October 6, 2026 local X Article, ‘I went from filming UGC in my room to 1.4B views in 90 days (full guide).’ The export names him only as Matt; no additional identity or connection to other Matt entities is established. He describes filming all UGC himself for a vibecoded app, then working on [[jenni-ai]] and multiple brands through [[hardlaunch]]. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

## Source-described trajectory and outcomes

- His own unnamed app: self-filmed bedroom-floor TikToks, $0 spend, sold after 6 months for $375,000; elsewhere he says the same system produced 30M views for an app he ran alone.
- Early failure: repeatedly 200 or 300 views, then 100k views but no signup or trial when he checked Stripe and RevenueCat. He uses this to separate reach from useful business outcomes.
- Jenni AI: says they crossed $1M MRR; a single video drove $10,000 in attributed revenue, distinct from another that drove $4k MRR and about $25,000 lifetime revenue.
- Broader operation: claims 1.4 billion views in 90 days, $1M+ MRR hit, zero paid spend and 7¢ CPI. He says a third-party analytics platform counted the views rather than his editable spreadsheet; no platform name, underlying report, or independent verification is supplied locally. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

## Operating position

His three levels are copy the working format, adapt the mechanism rather than the script, and propagate the winner across accounts while it has life. He prescribes 30 minutes a day scrolling inside the niche plus 30 minutes outside, says this has been his highest-leverage hour for two years, and treats taste/distribution rather than increasingly commoditized building as the moat. His ‘frontier 10%’ of taste includes recognizing formats, choosing adaptations, and writing hooks. These are his judgments, not established limits of AI or startup strategy. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

He rejects AI-written scripts, especially hooks, even after pipelines receive dozens of real human hooks: he says outputs sound slightly off and cannot reproduce how a 19-year-old describes a specific scenario. He prescribes formats and hooks rather than allowing creators to choose easy-to-film, high-view work; creators own execution. Filming, editing and posting are delegated first, content strategy last. His claim that 10% better content strategy produces 10x more views than most agencies is source-described and unverified. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

His scaling split is 20% at the front (format, principles, direction, what and why), 75% in the middle (briefing, filming, editing, scheduling and day-to-day creator management, delegated), and 5% at the end (his taste pass before shipping). He says he retains the first and last on every client campaign, not only interesting ones; the middle enables scale and the retained 25% makes it work. See [[format-propagation]] and [[ugc]] for the full operational protocol. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]
