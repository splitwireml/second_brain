# Chris (@everestchris6)

Chris (`@everestchris6`) is an X Article author documenting a source-described Reddit-first system for finding urgent problems, packaging answers as PDFs or newsletters, acquiring attention through Reddit, and automating research and drafting while keeping account-sensitive actions and DMs human-reviewed. The guide's revenue, reach, and account-performance claims are source-described, not independently audited. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-make-money-with-ai-agents-on-reddit-full-guide-2086455451429060984.md]

## Source-described operating model

- Read subreddits before building; repeated questions, tool requests, and “I wish there was” language are treated as demand signals.
- Use Google search operators such as `site:reddit.com/r/[subreddit] "how do i"`, filter to the past three months, and then use Apify MCP-connected research for the full post/comment corpus.
- Package the answer as a PDF guide or, for a smaller audience, a `$9/month` newsletter; use Claude or ChatGPT for research and drafting, Cursor with the Claude Code extension for the landing page, and a prebuilt skill file for the page stack/components.
- The source's unloopa example gives away a method using Google Maps to find local businesses, free tools to build their site, a Google Sheet the owner can edit, and an exact email; this is a source-specific example, not an inferred stack.
- Warm a real Reddit account through roughly fifteen genuine comments per day for two weeks; karma, comment karma, account age, subreddit rules, and shadowban visibility determine whether posting is possible.
- Keep Reddit posting and DMs human-controlled after testing the community manually; Hermes is used for scheduled research and draft preparation rather than automated submission.

## Evidence boundary

The source claims a Reddit-only marketing path, approximately `$1,000/day` results, large view/upvote totals, and an account that was eventually permanently banned. These are source claims. The local export does not establish a repeatable revenue rate, platform guarantee, safe account-transfer method, or reliable automated-posting configuration. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-make-money-with-ai-agents-on-reddit-full-guide-2086455451429060984.md]
