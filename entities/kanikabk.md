# Kanika

Kanika (@KanikaBK) is an X creator focused on practical AI education, especially multi-agent system design, orchestration patterns, and workflow implementation advice for business and content teams.

## Known For

- [[multi-agent-orchestration]] — full-course framing for turning one failing generalist agent into a coordinated team of narrow specialists
- [[agent-teams]] — emphasis on role clarity, critique loops, and structured handoffs rather than vague "everyone does everything" agent swarms

## Core Ideas

- Think in tasks, not job titles: narrow, testable agents beat broad role prompts.
- Add a dedicated critic/reviewer role instead of stopping at research → production → publish.
- Choose the lightest architecture that matches the workflow: sequential for pipelines, parallel for independent workstreams, hierarchical only when multiple pipelines must coordinate.
