# marc lou

Indie SaaS founder and educator.

## Background
- Broke in 2021
- Living in an underground apartment in South Korea
- By 2025 did over $1M in a single year from micro-SaaS products

## Products
- ShipFast (~$20k/mo)
- CodeFast (~$20k/mo)
- DataFast ($15.8k MRR)
- TrustMRR (~$35k/mo)

## Stats
- 35 startups shipped in total
- 5% hit rate
- 95% are dead or earning near-zero

## Philosophy
- "Build in public + ship + tweet"
- Ship more things, attach to none of them
- Each launch teaches you something and grows your audience
- The only way to lose is to keep polishing one idea instead of shipping the next
- In his 2026 post on [[viral-product-principles]], he argues that simplicity, visible pricing, emotional copy, and one-step conversion paths are the foundations of viral products
