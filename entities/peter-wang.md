# Peter Wang

Peter Wang (@BrainsAndTennis) is the author of the X article **“Building against the big labs that are trying to eat you.”** The article presents lessons from building the [[shortcut]] agent while Claude for Excel is a competitor, arguing that a focused workflow product can win through a tighter harness, model choice, and closer contact with users. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-building-against-the-big-labs-that-are-trying-to-e-2076767931053294017.md]

## Article contribution

Wang's central operating thesis is that a startup should optimize the complete workflow it owns rather than compete with a big lab on generality. The article emphasizes domain-specific prompts and tools, lean context, model routing across closed and open models, internal task evals, and a loop from real customer workflows back into product improvements. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-building-against-the-big-labs-that-are-trying-to-e-2076767931053294017.md]
