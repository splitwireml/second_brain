# CarReport

CarReport is the user's planned web app and digital product for U.S. used-car shoppers. Its intended job is to turn a dealership vehicle listing and available vehicle-history evidence into a detailed background report that helps a buyer decide whether to investigate, negotiate, or walk away.

## Product posture

The central commercial challenge is not explaining that the app uses AI. It is proving that the report is useful, traceable, and careful about uncertainty. Marketing should therefore make the **decision risk** visible while keeping every vehicle-specific claim tied to a source, a date, or an explicit "not found" result.

## Distribution implication

CarReport is a high-intent, trust-sensitive product. Short-form video can create awareness and demonstrate the report workflow, but conversion should happen through a real sample report, transparent methodology, and vehicle-specific evidence rather than a synthetic promise of a "clean" or "safe" car.
