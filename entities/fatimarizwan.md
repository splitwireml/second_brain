# Fama (@fatimarizwan)

Fama is an X article author and Okara operator who describes a repeatable, preparation-heavy product-launch system. Her local article attributes prior launch reach and visitor totals to the team's organic launch process; those metrics remain source-reported claims.

## Launch Practice

- **Product first:** show value within the first 30 seconds and reach the product's "aha" within 60 seconds; the article recommends timing five strangers using the product before launch.
- **One source of truth:** write a one-page press release and FAQs covering the customer problem, audience, offer, and price, then reuse it for the video, thread, influencer packet, and newsletter.
- **Pre-commit distribution:** recruit friends, power users, former colleagues, VCs, and relevant X influencers before launch, with calendar reminders and launch-minute coordination.
- **Hook and proof:** study successful launch openings, write many candidate hooks, and lead the launch thread/video with the product or problem rather than generic AI novelty.
- **Post-launch loop:** use the newsletter and direct follow-up to convert attention into customers, leads, or signups; treat features, milestones, and integrations as repeat launch opportunities.

The article's thresholds, reach figures, influencer counts, timing advice, and conversion claims are source-specific rather than independently verified.
