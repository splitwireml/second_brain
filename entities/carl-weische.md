# Carl Weische (@CarlWeische)

Carl Weische is the author of a source-described X Article on listicles as broad pre-sell pages for ecommerce cold traffic. The article explains how to organize pains, beliefs, objections, reasons, headlines, product mechanisms, benefits, scarcity, and split tests into a page that sends the reader to a PDP. The source's conversion and brand-performance claims are not independently verified.

## Evidence in this source

- Weische describes listicles as a versatile, high-converting pre-sell format and reports that the brands his team works with see conversion-rate lifts of roughly 20–100%; these are source-reported claims, not audited benchmarks.
- The source contrasts listicles with story-based advertorials, then gives a detailed multi-8-figure-brand sheets example: objection-ranked reasons, skim-proof headlines, a problem-to-pain-to-mechanism-to-benefit paragraph structure, urgency-led CTA wording, and continual split testing.
- The article frames a funnel as a sequence of pains and beliefs: the prospect has a serious problem, a solution exists, and the product is the best solution.
