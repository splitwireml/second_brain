# Viktor

Viktor is described by Machina as an AI employee that joins an existing Slack or Microsoft Teams workspace as a member, connects to business software through managed connectors, runs scheduled jobs from a sentence in chat, and starts with $100 in free credits. These are source-described product capabilities and a source-claimed credit offer, not independently verified product facts. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-build-and-scale-a-one-person-business-with--2081017272924361162.md]

## Source-described five-lane setup

The source's fastest path uses one channel per lane—content, projects, outreach, finance, and ads—with one pinned context message per channel covering what the business sells and to whom, that lane's deliverables, escalation rules, and rules that never bend. Viktor is invited to every channel; the outreach channel deliberately has no tool that can send. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-build-and-scale-a-one-person-business-with--2081017272924361162.md]

The five briefs are:

- **Content:** research what performed on X in the last 14 days around a niche, use real posts with real engagement, note each winner's angle, then draft 5 concrete posts and 1 thread on the week's topic for a reader skeptical of AI hype; deliver one organized thread, research first, and flag the strongest draft with a reason.
- **Projects:** read the channel context, post a weekly plan with each project's stage, what ships, and its biggest blocker; every workday at 8:00 post an under-8-line standup covering what moved yesterday, what is due today, and what is blocked; track posted status without asking.
- **Outreach:** research 10 real businesses matching the client profile, record what each does, the repetitive workload visible on its own website, and a confidence note; draft first touches for the top 5, opening with the workload spotted at that business, in two short paragraphs; drafts only, nothing sends, and sending remains the operator's decision.
- **Finance:** create a draft invoice in the billing tool for client, project milestone, and amount, post the link for review, and never finalize; every Friday at 16:00 post an under-10-line month-to-date summary of invoices issued, paid, and overdue with days outstanding.
- **Ads:** summarize the offer in two lines, propose a test plan with 2 specific audiences, 2 skeptical-of-AI ad angles per audience, and a split of the daily cap; after approval, build the campaign with everything PAUSED, and never go live without explicit go.

The source claims that $100 in free credits at viktor.com covers the whole five-lane build without a card; this remains a promotional claim. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-build-and-scale-a-one-person-business-with--2081017272924361162.md]
