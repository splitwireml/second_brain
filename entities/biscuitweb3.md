# Biscuit (@biscuitweb3)

Biscuit is the local-source author of a project-first learning-and-offer workflow: choose a small result that a buyer can evaluate, learn only the prerequisites needed for it, make a sample, obtain feedback, package a bounded offer, and put it in front of relevant people. The article treats AI as an on-demand tutor and reviewer rather than a substitute for understanding or delivery. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-the-fastest-way-to-learn-a-skill-that-makes-you-mo-2102426156699918496.md]

The source's seven-day challenge is explicitly a scope-and-feedback exercise, not a promise of professional competence or first payment in a week. It routes the learner from demand research to a sample, a clearly specified offer, a small paid trial where appropriate, and response-driven iteration. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-the-fastest-way-to-learn-a-skill-that-makes-you-mo-2102426156699918496.md]
