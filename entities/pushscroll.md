# Pushscroll

Pushscroll is Alejandro Sanchez's screen-time/app-blocking fitness app. The source claims the app generated more than **$1M in total iOS and Android proceeds** over one year and that users completed **28M push-ups** in total; these figures are source claims, not independently audited facts.^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-marketed-my-screen-time-app-to-1000000-with--2074524268285538350.md]

## Product-positioning mechanics

The product's marketing advantage is that the value proposition is visual: the viewer can understand the app from the push-up tracker plus app-blocking screen. The app also has a novelty edge because it combines doomscrolling reduction with a physical push-up behavior, creating a short-form video hook that can be demonstrated quickly.^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-marketed-my-screen-time-app-to-1000000-with--2074524268285538350.md]

## Growth notes

- Initial influencer spend reportedly failed, forcing the team into organic content creation.
- The article frames revenue-generating videos as ones that show the value proposition in the first 7-10 seconds and combine high-retention editing with storytelling.
- Comment-triggered DM automation via Manychat is presented as an engagement and install loop: comments create social proof, drive automated delivery, and may reinforce Instagram distribution.^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-marketed-my-screen-time-app-to-1000000-with--2074524268285538350.md]
