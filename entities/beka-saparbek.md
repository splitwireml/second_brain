# Shynggys Saparbek (@beka_saparbek)

Shynggys Saparbek is an iOS developer and X article author who describes a Reddit-first promotion playbook for an app related to AI image generation. The source reports 2,000 installs in 48 hours and 10,000 within a week from organic Reddit posts, followed by continued monthly acquisition after posting and advertising stopped; the performance and revenue figures are source claims, not independently audited. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-promote-your-app-on-reddit-2079081162014904353.md]

## Reddit promotion playbook

- Warm the account through genuine participation, relevant answers, comments, and useful non-promotional posts before sharing the app.
- Segment communities into platform, founder, and niche groups, then read each subreddit's promotional rules and culture.
- Study recent successful posts, preserve the underlying format rather than copying wording, and make the post valuable even without the app link.
- Adapt the same product to each community's reason for caring; stay transparent, answer comments, and avoid spam-like repetition.
- Use organically successful communities and messages as safer inputs for limited paid-ad experiments, then measure installs, revenue, referrals, and search rather than upvotes alone.
