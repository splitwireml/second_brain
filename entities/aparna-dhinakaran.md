# Aparna Dhinakaran

Aparna Dhinakaran is an X article author writing about AI engineering practice and the emerging vocabulary around agent loops. Her July 2026 article, co-authored with @seldo, maps the overloaded term "loop" into execution, task, product, system, and oversight layers instead of treating it as one generic autonomy pattern.^[raw/articles/xarticle-what-the-hell-is-a-loop-anyway-2073492320159510869.md]

## Contribution to the wiki

The durable contribution is a vocabulary bridge for [[loop-engineering]]: the article separates the agent's inner act-observe cycle from Ralph-style task reruns, software-factory product loops, autoresearch system loops, and the human oversight loop that sets goals, budgets, and culling rules.^[raw/articles/xarticle-what-the-hell-is-a-loop-anyway-2073492320159510869.md]
