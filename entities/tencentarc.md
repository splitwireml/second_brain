# Tencent ARC

Tencent ARC (Applied Research Center) is Tencent's research group for computer vision and generative media systems. In this wiki it is the lab behind [[pixal3d]] on [[huggingface]], and a research unit within [[tencent]].

## Overview

The Pixal3D model card ties Tencent ARC Lab to a SIGGRAPH 2026 single-image 3D generation system developed with collaborators from Tsinghua University and Victoria University of Wellington. The README also shows Tencent ARC shipping a public Hugging Face demo, inference code, training code, and a data-preparation toolkit in April–May 2026.

## Notable Work

- [[pixal3d]] — single-image 3D asset generation with explicit pixel-to-3D correspondences and PBR texture output.
