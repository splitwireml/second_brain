# Greg Isenberg

Greg Isenberg (@gregisenberg) is a founder and X creator who writes about AI-native products, mobile-app opportunities, agent businesses, and bootstrapped portfolio building. The July 2026 article is a source-described playbook for finding painful existing products, designing bounded agents, and taking repeated shots at small businesses rather than treating one startup as the only bet.^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-id-make-10-million-with-ai-agents-2076733920834371585.md]

## Source themes

- Find boring products with real revenue and poor user experience, then locate the AI-shaped gap.
- Start with a minimum viable agent, draft-and-approve controls, and a 50-example evaluation set before expanding autonomy.
- Use model chaining, reusable skills, and subagents to lower execution cost and turn one product into a repeatable portfolio process.
- Explore agent-native apps, iMessage interfaces, industry skills, and agent infrastructure as open surfaces in the proposed new operating system.
- Build distribution and UGC systems alongside the product so a solo operator can run more than one launch.

## AI-native services thesis (2026-09-20)

Greg Isenberg's September 2026 article frames an AI-native service as an outcome-selling business: agents perform most repeatable delivery while a small human team owns judgment, accountability, and the work that still requires a person. The source contrasts a roughly $10,000/year QuickBooks subscription with a roughly $120,000/year accountant and calls the service pool newly addressable; the dollar framing is source-described, not independently verified.^[raw/articles/xarticle-ai-native-services-a-100b-opportunity-2101760050108797268.md]

His proposed entry sequence is service first: define a checkable unit, serve five customers mostly by hand while reviewing every output, turn recurring mistakes into a niche rulebook, then fix scope, form-based intake, dashboard delivery, and pricing before optionally exposing the workflow as software. This complements [[ai-native-services-agency]], [[services-as-software]], [[agent-saas-playbook]], and the human-review boundary in [[human-in-the-loop]].^[raw/articles/xarticle-ai-native-services-a-100b-opportunity-2101760050108797268.md]
