# TikTok

Short-form video platform by ByteDance. Primary distribution channel for AI-generated UGC content and viral marketing campaigns.

## Key Facts
- Owned by ByteDance (same parent as Douyin in China)
- 1B+ monthly active users
- Algorithm-driven discovery — follows do not gate reach
- Primary format: 15s-10min vertical video
- Strong in 18-34 demographic globally; dominant in SE Asia, US, UK, Brazil
