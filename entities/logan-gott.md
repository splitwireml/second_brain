# Logan Gott

Logan Gott (@LoganTGott) is a founder-led LinkedIn growth operator at Gott Content. His ingested article frames LinkedIn authority as a complete B2B demand system: profile positioning, weekly content engine, lead-magnet capture, DM delivery, and outbound follow-up rather than isolated viral posts.

## LinkedIn Authority System

Gott's operating claim is that the post is only one component of the pipeline. The system starts with a narrow ICP and buyer-language extraction, rebuilds the profile as a landing page for that ICP, publishes a 7-post weekly mix across top/middle/bottom funnel, turns sales-call transcripts into objection-led content, and attaches a lead magnet plus LinkedIn outbound layer so attention converts into booked calls.

The article also positions [[claude]] Opus 4.8 as useful because higher self-critique and effort control help reject vague ICP answers, weak hooks, generic positioning, and unsupported claims before they reach the public content layer.
