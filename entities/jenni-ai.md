# Jenni AI

Jenni AI is the named brand for which [[mattgittleson]] says he ran UGC before expanding the same system across clients at [[hardlaunch]]. The source does not describe Jenni's product features or establish Matt's ownership/founder relationship; no such facts are inferred. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

## Source-described campaign evidence

Matt says Jenni crossed $1M MRR. He distinguishes one single video's $10,000 attributed revenue from a different video's $4k MRR and approximately $25,000 lifetime revenue; they are not the same video or the same revenue measure. The article supplies no revenue export, attribution window or method, retention model, or causal-control design. These remain source-described anecdotes. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

In the warmup workflow, Jenni creators had to reach 40 followers before posting anything promotional. The broader protocol is days 1–4 using the app for 15–30 minutes daily, properly watching/liking in-niche content and on Instagram following small likely-to-follow-back niche accounts; days 4–7 add three days of quick, high-viral-potential non-selling warmup posts, not the product or prescribed campaign format. This is an author-reported gate and procedure, not an official platform requirement.
