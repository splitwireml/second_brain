# Eyad Khrais

Eyad Khrais (@eyad_khrais) writes about applied AI engineering and agent production practice. His July 2026 X article frames the role as software engineering extended into probabilistic systems: evaluation suites, harness engineering, and coordination rules become the core craft.

## Applied AI engineering frame

Eyad's guide argues that the applied AI engineer does not merely prompt a model; they build the operating environment around it. The role starts by measuring behavior with evals, then builds the harness around tools, context, state, memory, guardrails, and loops, and finally treats multi-agent deployments as distributed systems rather than role-play prompts.
