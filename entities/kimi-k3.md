# Kimi K3

Kimi K3 is a Kimi model presented in Borja's July 2026 X article as the low-cost research worker for an AI-assisted broken-link outreach system. The model name, launch timing, benchmark score, and pricing figures below are source-reported rather than independently audited. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-kimi-k3-my-new-head-of-seo-link-outreach-broken-li-2079207506581045451.md]

## Role in the workflow

The source assigns Kimi K3 the high-volume hunting stage: crawl competitor backlink profiles, check 404 and soft-404 pages, inspect archives, rank prospects by referring domains, and help verify contacts. Claude remains responsible for deciding which opportunities to pursue, what replacement asset to build, and how to pitch it. In short: Claude thinks; Kimi digs. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-kimi-k3-my-new-head-of-seo-link-outreach-broken-li-2079207506581045451.md]
