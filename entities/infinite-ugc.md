# Infinite UGC

Infinite UGC is the AI UGC ad-production tool named in the local source. The article describes a built-in workflow for making long-form, two-person podcast scenes around a product, extending them beyond short-form tool limits, adding B-roll and captions, and exporting without a separate editor. Availability, feature behavior, and performance are source-described and unverified. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-do-6mmonth-with-my-ecom-brand-using-ai-podca-2080778980555133219.md]

## Source-described role

- Accepts a finished dialogue script and generates a two-person podcast-style scene.
- Supports extending the ad to several minutes, which the source presents as important for the format.
- Uses the Nebula agent to assemble multi-scene cuts with product shots and B-roll around the dialogue.
- Adds native TikTok captions and a reply-to-comment overlay before export.

The source does not independently establish Infinite UGC's product availability, pricing, generation quality, or the claimed production economics.
