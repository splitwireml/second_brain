# Connor Showler

SEO and marketing creator on X who publishes operator-style tactics for influencing search visibility across both classic SERPs and AI-mediated answer surfaces.

## Overview

This source frames [[connor-showler]] as an advocate of aggressive distribution: publish niche-relevant content on high-authority third-party domains, seed brand mentions across ranking surfaces, and use those surfaces as citation inputs for LLMs and Google AI Overviews. The operating assumption is that AI systems inherit a large share of their answers from what already ranks across the public web.^[raw/articles/ai-citation-seeding-connor-showler-2059259221238063421.md]

## Source-specific claims

### Confirmed from source
- He recommends posting niche content on Reddit, Quora, Medium, and other high-ranking third-party sites.^[raw/articles/ai-citation-seeding-connor-showler-2059259221238063421.md]
- He recommends reusing content from the primary site onto those external surfaces, then linking back where the author controls the profile or entity.^[raw/articles/ai-citation-seeding-connor-showler-2059259221238063421.md]
- In a follow-up reply, he says local-service variants of the tactic should target whatever already dominates the local SERP, especially BBB, local directories, and review sites.^[raw/articles/ai-citation-seeding-connor-showler-2059259221238063421.md]

### Likely but not independently verified here
- The causal claim that LLMs and Google AI Overviews largely average top-ranking results in the way the post suggests.
- The implied effectiveness of paid guest posts and tiered links for AI-citation capture rather than only for traditional SEO.
