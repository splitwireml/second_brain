# Florian Darroman

Florian Darroman (@floriandarroman) is a SaaS founder building distribb.io and writing about founder operating constraints, especially the isolation and decision fatigue that appear once a business is already generating revenue.

## Overview

This source is less a pure growth playbook than an argument that advanced founders need peer infrastructure, not beginner communities. Florian positions a paid mastermind as a high-accountability environment for founders already above $5K MRR who are trying to reach $100K.

## Key Content

- [[saas-founder-masterminds]] — founder peer groups as an operating layer for accountability, perspective, and faster correction of bad decisions
- Frames founder isolation as a structural bottleneck rather than a motivation problem
- Uses [[postiz]] founder [[nevodavid]] as an example of the kind of off-public knowledge transfer these groups can unlock
