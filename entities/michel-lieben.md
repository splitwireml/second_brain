## 2026-08-03 Cold Email Playbook

Michel's complete 2026 playbook presents outbound as a distribution system with seven ordered moves: run every campaign on hyper-personalization plus multi-channel rails; treat a real LinkedIn audience and its engagement as an asset; let AI handle pre-message labor while humans keep the words; invent proprietary intent signals; hand-write 35–50 messages before scaling; earn opens with expiring pattern interrupts; and connect recorder, sequencer, and CRM evidence through MCPs in Claude Code so each campaign improves. The source is a conversation with Charles Tenot, described as the CEO who grew Lemlist from $15M to $53M ARR, and Erwan, his VP of Growth; it frames their view around a platform seeing 20,000 signups/month and Michel's view from hundreds of B2B clients. These figures and role descriptions are source-described claims.^[raw/articles/xarticle-the-complete-cold-email-playbook-for-2026-its-a-di-2083199326004838508.md]

The playbook's concrete boundary is judgment: AI may scrape, enrich, build lists, score ICP fit, detect intent, analyze campaigns, find patterns, and draft follow-through, but the human writes the message, decides whether the message is good enough, and reviews the finished campaign. It names [[lemlist]] as the sequencer/platform used for engagement-derived targeting, spam-folder aggregation, warmup, and Claude-connected campaign execution, while leaving exact model versions, commands, configuration values, prompt text, file paths, and API schemas unspecified.^[raw/articles/xarticle-the-complete-cold-email-playbook-for-2026-its-a-di-2083199326004838508.md]
