# David Ch

David Ch ([@chhddavid](https://x.com/chhddavid/status/2100537116635672690)) is an X article author describing a source-reported manual study of 30,600 iOS App Store apps making $20k–$100k/month or more. He says he reviewed what those apps sell, their onboarding, marketing, and repeat-payment mechanics rather than using AI summaries or a large scraped dataset; revenue counts and the study's conclusions remain source-described and unverified.^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-manually-studied-30600-ios-apps-making-20k-100km-2100537116635672690.md]

## Operating framework

- **Market proof before features:** start from a consumer problem people already feel and already spend money on; inspect multiple competitors' revenue scale, customer complaints, and acquisition channels. The source uses Cal AI (food-photo calorie tracking) and Steve Hoyek's Journable (a simplicity-led calorie tracker) as examples, and argues that an idea explainable in a seven-second TikTok demo or naturally shareable result has a different growth profile. The named revenue figures are source claims.^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-manually-studied-30600-ios-apps-making-20k-100km-2100537116635672690.md]
- **Seven-to-fourteen-day first release:** aim to ship only onboarding, monetization, and the core app loop. The source names Claude Code and Shipper as development accelerators, but says Shipper needs product context—not a generic “build me a fitness app” instruction. Its explicit handoff is to download 5–10 leading category apps, use them end-to-end, capture onboarding, paywall, home screen, core feature, results, and interactions, infer the decisions beneath them, then provide Shipper screenshots, visual references, the user journey, opening action, subsequent action, and intended feel.^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-manually-studied-30600-ios-apps-making-20k-100km-2100537116635672690.md]
- **Conversion and distribution as linked systems:** onboarding should move a user from recognizing the problem to believing the app understands it, experiencing enough value to want the result, and then receiving a paywall. Study short-form hooks, first frame, time-to-product appearance, product demonstration, and CTA; provide proven creator examples, hooks, screen recordings, and download-producing angles rather than asking creators to invent strategy. The source's Cal AI experiment and creator-performance figures are source-described, not independent evidence.^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-manually-studied-30600-ios-apps-making-20k-100km-2100537116635672690.md]
- **Funnel diagnosis and scaling:** the article's marketing hierarchy is Tier 0 low views; Tier 1 views; Tier 2 views plus engagement; Tier 3 views, engagement, and downloads; Tier 4 adds conversions; Tier 5 adds low churn. It recommends fixing the stage that fails, producing 50–200 creative tests to reveal patterns, testing organic formats before paid spend, and then varying proven hooks, creators, openings, and demos. Its illustrative $1,000→500 installs→50 trials→20 paid funnel is an example, not a forecast.^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-manually-studied-30600-ios-apps-making-20k-100km-2100537116635672690.md]
