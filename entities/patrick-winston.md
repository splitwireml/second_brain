# Patrick Winston

Patrick Winston is presented in the local source as an MIT professor who led the MIT Artificial Intelligence Laboratory from 1972 to 1997, wrote a widely read AI textbook, and taught the annual “How to Speak” lecture. These biographical and reach claims are preserved as source-described context rather than independently verified fact. ^[raw/articles/thread-lumenxbt-2083603666603528596.md]

## How to Speak

The source says Winston gave the lecture every January for four decades, filmed it once in January 2018, and died eighteen months later; it later summarizes his death as occurring in 2019. The lecture's reusable rule set is filed in [[public-speaking-framework]]: do not read, be in the image, keep images simple, eliminate clutter, begin with empathy, end with a repeatable punch line, never open with a joke, and never end with “thank you.” ^[raw/articles/thread-lumenxbt-2083603666603528596.md]

The thread quotes Winston's opening line as: “Your success in life will be determined largely by your ability to speak, your ability to write, and the quality of your ideas. In that order.” The local source says the lecture is available through MIT OpenCourseWare and the textbook is free on his page, but it supplies no URLs and those destinations were not fetched. ^[raw/articles/thread-lumenxbt-2083603666603528596.md]
