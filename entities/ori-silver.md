# Ori Silver

Ori Silver (@OriSilver) is an X article author who presents a source-described MaxFusion AI workflow for turning proven static ads into six-second animated statics.

## Source contribution

- Describes an own-ad mode that preserves a winning static's layout, offer, copy, colors, and logo while adding element-by-element stop-motion.
- Describes a competitor-swap mode that borrows a working format skeleton while replacing the competitor's brand-specific material.
- Places three approval gates around extraction, the composed still, and the audio/video generation step; the result can be delivered as video or GIF.

The platform availability, free-skill promotion, and any performance or marketing advantage remain source-described claims rather than independently verified facts.
