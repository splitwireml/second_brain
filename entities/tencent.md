# Tencent

Chinese tech conglomerate (SEHK: 0700). In this wiki, Tencent appears through multiple AI efforts spanning foundation models, embodied AI, translation, and 3D generation.

## Overview

Tencent is a Chinese multinational conglomerate known for social media (WeChat), gaming, cloud services, and AI research. The HY Vision Team focuses on vision foundation models while Robotics X works on physical AI systems. The new [[tencentarc]] ingest adds another visible branch: computer-vision and generative-media work around [[pixal3d]].
