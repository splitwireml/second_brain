# Juniko

X creator [@junikoETH](https://x.com/junikoETH) who publishes practical workflows for building and monetizing synthetic AI influencers.

## Article Method

Juniko's article describes a reference-driven production pipeline:

1. **Appearance:** combine donor facial features into a consistent identity with NanoBanana.
2. **Body and scene:** preserve the identity reference while transferring pose, clothing, environment, lighting, and composition from another image.
3. **Video:** replace the subject in a video reference with the generated influencer in KlingAI.
4. **Metadata:** the article recommends replacing or spoofing metadata on generated media before social upload; this is a source-reported tactic, not an independently verified or platform-approved practice.

The proposed monetization routes include an Instagram persona selling photos and videos through Fanvue or Stacked, and a sports persona selling training programs or online coaching.
