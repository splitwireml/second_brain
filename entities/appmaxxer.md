# Appmaxxer

Appmaxxer ([@appmaxxing](https://x.com/appmaxxing)) is an X creator and indie iOS operator documenting high-margin App Store portfolio building. The July 2026 article frames "appmaxxing" as an ASO/SEO/AEO-first alternative to UGC- or paid-ads-heavy mobile app growth: build many focused products, reuse architecture and components, and let agents handle repeatable research, metadata, screenshot, setup, and QA work.^[raw/articles/xarticle-ios-apps-the-full-framework-to-appmaxxing-with-70--2074972100201177550.md]

## Operating pattern

- Runs a portfolio-style iOS app factory rather than a single flagship app.
- Uses Claude or Codex with Apple/App Store Connect APIs, RevenueCat or Superwall, keyword tools such as Astro, and reusable house-style memory to scaffold, configure, localize, screenshot, and iterate apps.^[raw/articles/xarticle-ios-apps-the-full-framework-to-appmaxxing-with-70--2074972100201177550.md]
- Treats first-party App Store data and keyword research as more reliable than generic X advice; the public thread is positioned as a framework, not audited revenue proof.
