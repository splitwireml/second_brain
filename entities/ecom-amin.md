# Amin (@eCom_Amin)

Amin is an X creator and e-commerce marketing operator. In this X Article, he documents a source-described workflow for using Claude Fable 5 to produce Google Ads advertorials matched to search intent and the traffic's awareness level. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-cook-killer-google-ads-advertorials-with-fab-2076724912937750754.md]

## Evidence in this source

- Amin reports reducing one advertorial from 4–6 hours of manual work to under 30 minutes; the claimed quality and performance improvements are not independently verified.
- His method uses keyword-to-awareness mapping, a six-block prompt architecture, and three revision passes for the hook, mechanism, and CTA.
- He describes multiplying one product into 3–5 advertorial angles and reports source-claimed ROAS improvements from PDP campaigns; those figures remain source claims, not audited benchmarks.
