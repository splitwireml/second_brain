# Postiz

Agentic social media scheduling tool that enables users to automate posting to 30+ social media platforms. Hooks into AI agents for autonomous content scheduling and, in later sources, appears as the distribution layer in clipping-page workflows.

## Overview

Postiz reached $100k MRR as a bootstrapped, one-person company with no investors or capital raised. Growth was linear-to-exponential, culminating in a spike to 700 concurrent trials before settling into strong MRR.

## Technical Approach

- **Distribution engine**: turns finished content into a multi-platform schedule rather than leaving assets in a folder
- **MCP integration**: later sources describe Postiz as an MCP-connected scheduling surface that Claude can drive directly
- **Reliability focus**: job queues, observability, and stable posting matter because one failed post can break the value proposition

## Distribution Strategy

1. **Open-source first**: posted regularly on Reddit /r/selfhosted
2. **Developer community**: contributed code, gave fast feedback, helped build product
3. **Brand building**: strong name = strongest marketing
4. **Platform diversity**: Reddit, TikTok, X articles, Lemmy, Product Hunt
5. **Agentic integration**: becomes more valuable when paired with content-generation or clipping pipelines
