# content-strategy

Planning and executing content creation across platforms to attract and engage a target audience. Referenced in [[chatgpt-seo-ranking]] and [[intentional-relationship-building]].

## Entertainment-led ecommerce creative

Gurnoor Singh's short bookmark frames **entertainment that sells** as an emerging ecommerce-ad principle: study a product ad for its entertainment value, not only its sales message. The local export does not preserve the Pureskin ad itself, so this is a narrow source observation rather than a complete creative framework.^[raw/articles/thread-gurnoor__-2075063658523312256.md]

## Dickie Bush multi-platform cadence

Dickie Bush's source describes a six-platform content cadence organized around three rules: use one core CTA per platform, build the cadence before filling it, and do not copy a mature 160-post system before establishing a smaller 90-day habit. X is the long-form idea source and discovery surface; LinkedIn is the steady professional-growth channel; Instagram tests trial reels, carousels, and stories; Substack Notes compounds newsletter subscriptions; Threads mirrors repurposed short-form; and YouTube carries durable long-form video. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-maximize-your-content-output-across-all-soc-2079228257401184282.md]

The production model separates **created**, **produced**, and **reposted** work: about 20 net-new pieces are transformed into a much larger cross-platform output. This makes cadence a resource-allocation and repurposing problem, not a demand for 160 original ideas. The article’s platform and algorithm observations remain source-reported.
