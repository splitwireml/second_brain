# Brannon Hogue

Brannon Hogue (@brannonhogue) is an X creator whose local source describes a qualification-first cold-email process: validate and reduce the list, research prospect signals, tier leads, and reserve a concrete free offer for budget-qualified prospects.^[raw/articles/post-brannonhogue-youre-supposed-to-throw-away-75-of-your-cold-email-2083597307375735213.md]

## Source-described cold-email process

- Start with 5,000 emails.
- Validate the emails twice and exclude catch-alls; the source reports roughly 2,900 valid emails.
- Research prospects with “cheap ai search”; Gemini or another research AI is named as capable of finding relevant signals.
- Score prospects into tiers 1, 2, and 3, then remove tier 3; the source reports roughly 1,200 tier-1/tier-2 leads.
- Example signals for a website-builder offer: a poor website, growing staff count, and active growth-minded hiring.^[raw/articles/post-brannonhogue-youre-supposed-to-throw-away-75-of-your-cold-email-2083597307375735213.md]

## Offer and qualification boundary

- Make the offer so valuable that prospects cannot pass it up; the source recommends giving away a solution to a main business problem for free, not a generic “free audit.”
- Be selective about freeloaders. Solving one problem for free may reveal a second problem that becomes the paid engagement.
- Scoring is also a budget filter: avoid giving free services to prospects with no budget.^[raw/articles/post-brannonhogue-youre-supposed-to-throw-away-75-of-your-cold-email-2083597307375735213.md]

The source does not specify the validation service, catch-all detection method, research interface/API, model version, scoring rubric, prompts, exact offer, cadence, sending infrastructure, or measured conversion results. The counts and outcomes are source-described, not independently verified.^[raw/articles/post-brannonhogue-youre-supposed-to-throw-away-75-of-your-cold-email-2083597307375735213.md]
