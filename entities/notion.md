## Overview

**Notion** is an all-in-one workspace for notes, docs, wikis, and project management. Known for its empty state design with a blinking cursor and subtle menu options that guide new users.

## Key Facts

- Founded: 2013
- Headquarters: San Francisco, CA
- Type: SaaS productivity tool

## Empty State Design

Notion's empty page shows a blinking cursor and a subtle menu of options, giving new users immediate clarity on what to do next while feeling like infinite possibility.
