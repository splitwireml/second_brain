# Matt Chow (@mattchowx)

Matt Chow is the author of the local X Article and a source-described co-founder of Trope, which launched on Y Combinator. He documented how he and his co-founder built Trope's 75-second launch video with Claude Code, Fable 5, and Remotion rather than commissioning an agency. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-we-made-our-yc-launch-video-in-15-days-with-fa-2075672770483269788.md]

## Evidence in this source

- The article attributes the launch-video workflow and cost/time figures to Chow's team.
- Chow's co-founder Victor supplied the storyboard PDF and product code to the Fable 5 session.
- The source describes the team's use of up to six parallel Fable 5 sessions and a final DaVinci Resolve pass.
