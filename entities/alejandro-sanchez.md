# Alejandro Sanchez

Alejandro Sanchez ([@skyirezumi](https://x.com/skyirezumi)) is the founder/operator behind [[pushscroll]], a screen-time and app-blocking fitness app. His July 2026 X article documents a source-claimed path from a $0 marketing budget to more than $1M in total iOS and Android proceeds by shifting from failed influencer spend to organic short-form video production.^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-marketed-my-screen-time-app-to-1000000-with--2074524268285538350.md]

## Operating playbook

- Treat the app idea itself as the first marketing asset: the value proposition must be visual, novel, and understandable before a viewer reads explanatory text.
- Study high-performing creators and competitors to learn hook construction, watch-time retention, and delivery rather than assuming organic video is luck.
- Adapt proven formats from competitor and niche videos, but add a novel spin instead of copying pixel-for-pixel.
- Measure marketing by revenue and high-intent user spikes, not views alone; the article criticizes vanity-view formats that fail to convert.
