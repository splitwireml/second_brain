# An AI influencer who won't let you die broke (TBD)

An AI influencer who won't let you die broke (TBD)

Disclaimer: if you are lazy and not disciplined, then even a genie's lamp will not help you =]

And so let's begin...

Creating AI influencers  is a profitable niche, which requires a competent technical approach, an understanding of social media algorithms and the right choice of tools. In this article we will try to break down the whole process.

In the future you will be able to monetize the created influencer in very many ways and it depends only on your imagination.

You can create an Instagram blogger and later sell her photos and videos on Fanvue or Stacked.

You can also create a sports blogger and sell training programs and online coaching.

---

Follow my step by step guide :

1// Development of the model's appearance.

The main mistake of beginners is the attempt to make the influencer too unique by means of scars, skin spots or unrealistic facial features.

Even if it is an Instagram model, such photos will not sell.

There is no need to try to create a face from scratch. Go to Pinterest, find two different girls (for example, you like the nose shape and squinting look of one, and the hair of the other) and combine them into one using a prompt in NanoBanana.

Prompt example:

```
Integrate a face into an existing scene.
Substitute the face in the reference image with the face from the donor image.
The objective is a seamless merge: the new face must inherit the exact expression, pose,
 and lighting interaction from the reference, while its color attributes (hair and eyes) are 
adapted from the donor for a perfectly harmonious and natural result.
```

You can add a tattoo or something else as a feature.

2// Body creation

Again we go to Pinterest and look for a body that will suit our influencer. Then we go to NanoBanana and generate a body using a prompt.

Prompt example :

```
Replace the woman in the second reference image with the woman from the first reference image.
The first image is the identity reference and must be strictly preserved: keep her face, body
shape, exact proportions, and silhouette unchanged. Do not modify anatomy, do not stretch or resize
body parts. Use the outfit from the second reference image. The clothing, styling, and fit must match
the second image exactly. Transfer the woman from the first image into the pose of the second image,
matching body position precisely while keeping original proportions. The second image defines the environment:
keep the same background, camera angle, framing, perspective, and composition. Match the lighting from the
second image exactly: same light direction, intensity, shadows, highlights, and color grading. Match the
image quality of the second photo: same resolution, sharpness, noise level, skin detail, lens characteristics,
and overall realism. Do not enhance or degrade quality replicate it exactly. Ensure seamless blending into the
scene with correct depth and perspective. No stylization, no reinterpretation, no body reshaping.
Photorealistic, natural result good quality, delete tattoo on hand, no text
```

3// Video creation

We go to KlingAI and drop in a video reference and our influencer. We write a prompt to replace the girl.

4// Metadata replacement

When you have generated  a photo or video through AI there is absolutely no metadata on it. This is a big red flag for social networks. They might stop promoting you or block you altogether.

We go to any metadata removal site and upload our influencer photo or video and then a random photo from the phone. The site will spoof the metadata and everything will be fine.
