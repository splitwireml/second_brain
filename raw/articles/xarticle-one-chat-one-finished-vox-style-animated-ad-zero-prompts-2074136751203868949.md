You've seen the paper-collage explainer format taking over your feed.

## What the skill does

Load it up, describe your product.

It runs you through a quick intake. Got a product photo? Want a character fronting the ad, or a pure faceless explainer? 9:16 or 16:9? Done, that's the whole interview.

Drop in a product image and it gets wired into every single clip as an actual reference file, so the product stays pixel-consistent from the first second to the last.

One example is the Hevy app. Just give it an actor image and it does the whole thing for you.

Different styles of ads attract attention. that's the key here.

Choose mascot mode and you hand it a character image. The skill turns that character into a paper-cutout host who carries the whole ad on camera. Choose faceless and you get the pure Vox treatment instead: zero talking heads, all illustrations, arrows, and on-screen labels doing the selling.

From there it drafts the narration, maps out every visual beat, and puts the complete plan in front of you once. You approve, it generates every clip inside MaxFusion AI.

Each 10-second clip picks up from the final frame of the one before it, so the finished piece plays as one unbroken video. You touch nothing. Claude runs the whole chain.

You return to a done ad.

## Why Omni Flash and not Seedance

Every other creator demoing this format defaults to Seedance. Bad move. For your wallet and your calendar.

Per clip, Seedance costs more. It renders slower. And it fails hardest at exactly the 2 things this format lives on: legible text on screen, and clean spoken narration.

Strip those 2 things out of a Vox explainer and there's no explainer left. Labels and voice-over ARE the format. Every time the model butchers them, you re-roll, and every re-roll is credits and tokens down the drain on footage you'll never use.

Omni Flash keeps the text sharp, keeps the speech clean, keeps the look locked across an entire chain of clips, and does it in a fraction of the render time. For this one format, there's no debate.

And when a re-roll does happen? Cheaper, sure, but the real win is how fast it comes back.

## Fixing the narrator voice

There's one quirk to know about: Omni bakes the narration into the video itself, and it won't promise you the identical voice from clip to clip.

The skill pins the narrator's gender, so you'll never hear a woman in clip 1 and a man in clip 2. But 3 clips can still come back with 3 different-sounding male narrators.

Here's the fix:

Stitch all the clips together in CapCut.

Export the full video.

Upload it to MaxFusion AI.

Open the contextual menu and choose Replace Voice.

Pick a voice from the library, or from your own cloned voices.

One narrator, start to finish, in a single pass.
