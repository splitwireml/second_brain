METHOD 1: AI UGC -> THE RECOMMENDATION VIDEO

AI UGC content looks like a real person recommending a product.

a woman in her 40s talking about the glokore LED mask she's been using for 3 weeks. a man explaining why he switched from his $400 smartwatch to the herz P1 smart ring. someone in a living room holding a bareearth grounding bed sheet explaining what it does and why they bought it.

none of these people are real. all of these videos look real.

the algorithm on tiktok and instagram cannot tell the difference which means it distributes them exactly as it would distribute content from a genuine creator.

the omni 1.1 workflow:

go to pinterest and find a clear, natural photo matching your target demographic. face and features clearly visible. ordinary environment, the more natural the setting, the more authentic the content feels.

drop the image into chatgpt: "give me a very detailed JSON prompt to recreate this image 1:1."

open a new chat, select create image, paste the JSON and add: "make sure it's 9:16."

take the generated image into omni 1.1 as your starting frame.

get claude to write the script. the brief: hook addresses a specific problem the viewer already has before the product is mentioned, product introduced as a personal discovery not a promotion, CTA feels like a recommendation not a pitch.

generate for $0.50.

scripts that convert:

for the scalp massager ($73 payout): "my hair was thinning and i was genuinely starting to panic. a girl i follow mentioned this. been using it for 5 weeks. link in bio."

for the glokore LED mask ($73 payout): "three dermatologist appointments and $400 later someone showed me this. i've been using it for 4 weeks. i wish i'd found it two years ago."

for the herz P1 smart ring ($52 payout): "i was spending £300 a year on GP appointments just to check my heart rate. a friend mentioned this. i've been wearing it for 6 weeks. i haven't been back since."

notice the pattern. specific relatable problem. genuine-sounding discovery. credibility built before the product is named. soft CTA at the end.

claude writes these in under 3 minutes when the product details and target demographic are in the project brief. generate 5 to 6 variations with slightly different character starting frames and run all of them, the data shows you which avatar converts best.
