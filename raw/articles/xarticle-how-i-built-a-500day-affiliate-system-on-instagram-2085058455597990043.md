Most important part of converting your traffic is writing scripts that activates action

The action you want them to do in your video is simply create enough curiosity and belief that they want to find out more in the free guide, so they comment

You are not trying to sell them in the actual video. This is what the emails are for, you can get a little specific and market harder / more aggressively in the emails

In order to write good scripts that achieves this trust and authority you must do correct research. This is a simple guide on how I do my research:

1. Go to reddit and search for you specific problem e.g. losing weight for women going through menopause. and you want to just simply start copy and pasting threads and comments into a messy google doc

2. Then take this google doc and feed it to Claude and ask it to make a research brief for you using the raw data doc. Making avatars for you, top objections to your product, biggest struggles, the way they talk about they're problem

3. Then feed this to Claude and give it this prompt:

" give me 25 different 8-10 second hooks based on the document I gave you for UGC using captivating initial psycological loops like:

- "This may surprise you..."

- "I just figured this out..."

- "I wish I knew this earlier..."

- "Pause for a second..."

- "Here's the real truth..."

- "Ever notice this pattern?"

Then ask it "what are the 10 best that you believe would retain customer attention" Then ask it to give you a 40 second body for these hooks

This will give you decent scripts but you still need to use your brain to change it up but its good for coming up with angles / hooks
