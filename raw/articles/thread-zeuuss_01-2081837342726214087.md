Build a playable web game.

Title: Sands of the Lost Carpet (original — no existing IP)

Genre / core loop: a fast, stylized third-person / 2.5D endless flying-runner. The player rides a magic carpet, dodging obstacles, collecting gold coins and gems, and racing to escape a collapsing cavern and soar out over a desert oasis. Score = distance + coins; one run ends on 3 hits or a crash.

Controls (keep simple, works on desktop + mobile):

Left / Right (A-D or arrow keys, or tilt/drag on touch) — steer the carpet
Up / Down (W-S or swipe) — climb / dive
Space / tap — BOOST (short speed burst, limited energy meter)

Gameplay:

Auto-forward flight at steady speed; player weaves to avoid falling rocks, stone pillars, and molten bursts inside the cavern.
Collect floating gold coins (score) and rare blue gems (bonus + refill boost).
After ~30 seconds the carpet bursts out of a giant beast-mouth cave into a bright oasis — obstacles change to palm trees, waterfalls, and city spires.
Difficulty ramps: faster speed, denser obstacles over time.

Visual style: vibrant stylized-cartoon (Pixar-meets-Fortnite), saturated gold-teal-magenta palette, warm sunset light, glowing bloom, particle sparkles. Two environments: (1) collapsing golden treasure cavern, (2) lush oasis valley with turquoise river, waterfalls, and a domed palace-city at sunset.

Character (fully original): a young acrobat hero — teal hood and vest, red sash, tan trousers — riding an ornate patterned carpet, with a small original critter companion clinging on. No Disney designs.

HUD: score top-right, coin counter, a BOOST energy bar, 3 hit-point hearts top-left, distance meter.

UI: simple start screen (title + "Tap to Fly"), game-over screen with score + "Retry" button, rising difficulty, a coin pickup sound and boost whoosh.

Make it smooth, juicy, and instantly playable in the browser. Original assets and characters only — no logos, no brand marks, no real game titles.
