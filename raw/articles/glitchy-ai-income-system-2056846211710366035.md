## Part 1: Select what to make money with

First you need somewhere to send users that actually pays you.

Glitchy is an affiliate network that pays on average $0.80 every time someone clicks your link. No product. No customer service. No fulfillment. Sign up, pick an offer, get people to click your link, get paid.

Sign up for Glitchy: https://app.glitchy.com/ref?id=3396

After you're signed up, you need to select a glitchy offer. I recommend scrolling TikTok looking for trending products, topics or viral-moments and select an offer based on what's already viral and in demand.

From the viral videos you found, you will need to download their scripts for the next step. You can do this through a TikTok transcription tool, there are hundreds out there for free.

This way you don't re-invent the wheel, but use what's already working to make money.

## Part 2: Setup so you make money with the offer you selected

Your Glitchy offer you just selected has all the information you need sitting right there. The product, the audience, the pain point it solves etc.

Most people never use that information systematically. They read it once and then try to come up with content from scratch anyway.

You instead feed your Glitchy offer details into an Adaptive agent as a brief.

If you're unfamiliar with Adaptive, it's an AI agent platform where you describe what you want in plain English, connect your tools, and it builds and runs the workflow 24/7.

In this case, the agent pulls your brief about the offer, analyzes what angles are working in your niche right now, and outputs structured scripts ready to hand off to your content tool.

Sign up for Adaptive: https://www.adaptive.ai/

Here is the exact prompt I used to build this:

"Build me an AI agent that takes a product brief as input. The brief will include the offer name, target audience, core pain point + scripts from already viral TikTok videos. From this brief the agent should generate 10 hook angles based on what is currently performing in this niche. Then write 3 scripts per angle. Each script needs a hook in the first 2 seconds, a middle section explaining the offer value simply, and a CTA pointing to the landing page. Output everything in a structured format on the agent dashboard."

Five minutes later the agent was live which we will base our entire system on,

This prompt gave me this dashboard that my agent reports to, both in terms of content production and analysis (which we'll explain in the next step)

## Part 3: Turn scripts into videos

Now you have scripts built on proven angles in your niche.

Take the three strongest hook variations and drop them into MakeUGC. MakeUGC lets you generate UGC style videos with AI avatars in minutes. Pick an avatar that fits your niche, drop in the script, and get back a video that looks like a real person filmed it on their phone.

No camera. No face. No waiting weeks for a creator to deliver.

Finance or business niche, pick someone credible and professional. Lifestyle or wellness, pick someone relatable and casual. Congruence between avatar and offer is what makes it feel authentic.

## Part 4: The quality check

Before anything goes live, one simple check.

Would you stop scrolling for this. Would someone in your target audience watch it all the way through.

Not is this good enough. Is it actually good.

If yes, post it. If no, feed that back into the agent and it adjusts the next batch.

## Part 5: Post everywhere

TikTok. Instagram Reels. YouTube Shorts. Facebook. Snapchat Spotlight. All of them.

Same content, every platform, minutes of work. Every account is another traffic source pointing to the same landing page pointing to the same Glitchy link.

If you do not have accounts yet create them and warm them up for a day or two before posting anything promotional. Engage with content in your niche first. Then go full send.

Landing page in bio on every account. Post 3x a day minimum.

Now here is where most people stop. They post, check the numbers, shrug, and move on.

This system does not stop there.

## Part 6: Iteration

Once your videos go live, performance data starts coming in.

Views. Watch time. Glitchy clicks. Landing page conversions.

You feed that back into the Adaptive agent (or have it scrape this automatically). The agent reads the performance data and generates iteration briefs automatically.

So this is now the beautiful part of this system. It AUTOMATICALLY changes itself and the future content based on what works for you and for others. So you maximize your chances of going viral without having to spend hours researching.

Hook underperformed? The agent does not just tell you. It generates replacement hooks based on the same format that worked best across your other posts.

High views but low Glitchy clicks? The agent shifts the next batch of scripts toward conversion language. Tightens the CTA placement. Sharpens the middle section.

Strong clicks but low views? The agent focuses the next hooks on what stopped the scroll in your best performing video.

You are not guessing anymore. You are running a system that tells you exactly what to make next based on what already worked.
