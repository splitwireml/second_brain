# how i do $6M/month with my ecom brand using AI "podcast ads"...

how i do $6M/month with my ecom brand using AI "podcast ads"...

podcast ads are the format PRINTING for my brands right now.

two people on a podcast set, talking about the product like it came up mid-conversation.

they read as content, so people watch them all the way through instead of scrolling past.

this is how i make them with ai, start to finish...

---

## why podcast ads work for ecom:

the whole game in ecom is ad creative people actually watch.

podcast ads win because they read as NATIVE content:

- half of tiktok and reels is podcast clips already, so the format blends into the feed

- two people talking reads as a real conversation, so the guard drops

- the product comes up naturally inside the chat, so nobody feels SOLD to

- the podcast frame makes the speakers look like they know their shit

- longer watch time feeds the algorithm more of your impressions for the same spend

for a physical product this matters even more.

a plain talking head ad can only describe the product.

a podcast clip gives you a few minutes to raise the problem, react to it, and talk through the result like two people who actually use the thing.

that back and forth does the persuasion for you, since a viewer believes two people agreeing far more than one person pitching.

---

## writing the podcast ad script:

the script carries the ad.

the structure i use:

- a HOOK in the first 3 seconds, meaning a bold claim, a problem, or a controversial line

- one person raises the problem, the other reacts and gets curious

- the product comes in as the answer, mid conversation

- they talk through how it works and the exact result it got

- one of them handles the obvious objection out loud

- a soft CTA at the end pointing to where to buy

the two character dynamic is what sells.

make one person the skeptic and one the person who found the fix, so the skeptic asks the questions your viewer is already thinking.

when the skeptic gets won over inside the clip, the viewer gets won over with them.

weave the product in as the NATURAL answer to the problem, so it lands as a recommendation between two people.

write it how people actually talk, with filler words, interruptions, and reactions in it, since a script that reads too clean kills the whole illusion.

i write 20 hook variations for every product before touching a single video, because the hook decides whether anyone sees the rest.

claude spins the scripts and the hooks for me, since it writes dialogue that sounds HUMAN and gives me volume fast.

---

## creating it with ai:

once the script is done, the video takes about FIVE minutes.

i use infinite ugc for the podcast ads since it has a feature built for this exact format.

the workflow:

- drop the script in and it generates the two-person podcast scene

- extend the ad as long as you want, so a 3 minute podcast ad is fine

- the nebula agent builds the multi scene cut, dropping in b roll and product shots around the dialogue on its own

- caption it with the native tiktok captions built in

- export it finished, with no jump to another editor

the length part matters more than anything.

most ai ugc tools cap you around 15 seconds, and a podcast ad NEEDS a couple of minutes to breathe.

that cap alone kills the format on other tools, which is why the no limit extend is the feature i care about most.

the b roll is handled inside the tool too.

the nebula agent generates the product shots and cutaways around the dialogue on its own,

so the whole ad comes out of one place with no second editor.

claude is the one outside tool i lean on, for writing the scripts and the 20 hook variations per product.

the built in editor then captions it and adds a reply-to-comment overlay before export.

## testing and scaling:

one good podcast ad on its own means nothing.

the money is in VOLUME and TESTING.

how i run it:

- make 20+ variations of every script, swapping the hook and the opening 3 seconds

- upload them into meta and tiktok ads manager in batches

- run them broad with a small budget per ad set, and let the algorithm find the winner

- read CTR first, then hold rate, then roas as the spend climbs

- kill anything under a 1% ctr by day 3

- take the two that print and make 20 more of only those, changing one variable at a time

the reason this works is speed and cost together.

a human ugc creator charges around $300 a video and takes nine days to deliver one angle.

i make 30 podcast ads in an AFTERNOON for a flat monthly fee, then test all 30 at once.

when a format stops working, i swap the hook or the angle and refill the pipeline the same day.

creative velocity was always the real bottleneck behind my numbers, and the volume this unlocked is what scaled me to $6m a month.
