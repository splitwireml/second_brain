2026 AI Visibility Audit, Do or Die

🚨 If you want AI recommending your brand, but:

Plenty of teams are about to burn a year building a separate "AI search strategy" they don't need.

For years, the SEO playbook barely moved:

↳ Pick a keyword and chase the #1 spot.

↳ Work it into the page, then wait on Google.

↳ Publish and keep refreshing the rankings.

↳ Treat raw traffic as the finish line.

↳ Bolt on a fresh plan every time the channel shifted.

## So before you hire or build a second strategy, run a quick audit:

1️⃣ Fire up ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini.

↳ Ask the same thing a few times. The answer shifts each round.

2️⃣ Type the questions a buyer asks when they're ready to purchase.

↳ Skip research queries. Go for buying ones.

3️⃣ Watch which brands the model recommends.

↳ If yours never shows, note who does.

4️⃣ Track the sources those answers pull from.

↳ News sites, Reddit, LinkedIn, podcasts.

↳ Where you're absent is a gap.

5️⃣ Map where you show up and where you don't.

↳ That gap is your starting line.

6️⃣ Rank those sources by how often models quote them.

↳ And by how realistically you can get in. That's your priority list.

7️⃣ Then fix your own pages.

↳ Clear answers, clean formatting, the proof a model needs to cite you.
