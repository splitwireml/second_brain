# how to put b2b lead gen on full autopilot with one AI agent + free data

how to put b2b lead gen on full autopilot with one AI agent + free data

somewhere right now a founder is paying over $1,000 a month for apollo, zoominfo and clay

then paying a $140k SDR to send 50 messages a day off those exact lists

meanwhile a scraper hes never heard of is pulling 350,000 leads a day from public data that costs nothing, and an agent is writing the copy, sending it, and booking the call before he finishes his coffee

its the same outbound either way. one version just costs a salary and a stack of subscriptions to run

im going to show you how to build the second one. the free data layer, the agent stack that runs the desk, and how the calendar fills itself while you sleep

no apollo, zoominfo or $140k hire is needed.

no agency babysitting your campaign once a week either.

---

## how outbound usually gets built

almost every piece of it is rented.

rent a list from apollo. rent enrichment from zoominfo. rent sequences from instantly. rent clay to glue it all together.

thats over a grand a month before a single message goes out, and none of those tools actually talk to each other.

then they hire a body to run it.

fully loaded, an SDR runs about $139k a year, takes 3 months to ramp, and quits inside two years. youre training someone to leave.

or they hand the whole thing to an agency. $8k a month, 3 month lock-in, and behind the curtain its instantly plus apollo plus an upwork va on shared infrastructure, checked once a week.

youre paying $8k for a $1k setup and a middleman who watches.

every version of this has the same two problems baked in...

the data is recycled and the labor is human.

recycled data means youre landing on prospects already buried under the 120 other sales emails they get every week, which is exactly why reply rates across the whole industry have slipped under 1.2% and keep falling.

human labor means you cap out around 50 messages a day and a salary that eats the margin you were trying to grow.

change those two things and most of the problem disappears.

---

## where the leads come from

apollo isnt magic.

its public information that someone scraped, cleaned, and now rents back to you and ten thousand other people for $99 a month.

the same information is sitting at the source, and you can pull it directly.

public company data, google maps, public registries and filings, and a stack of unconventional sources nobody in outbound bothers to touch.

its all sitting in the open. our scrapers run 24/7 in the cloud pulling over 350,000 leads a day from sources like these, and not one of them comes from a $500-a-month vendor.

the only thing you genuinely pay for is turning a name into a verified email, and even that doesnt need zoominfo.

cheap unlimited email finding (blitz api, mailtester ninja) does the same job for pennies on the dollar.

the result is clean, fresh data the other 200 agencies dont have, because theyre all still fighting over the same apollo export.

and the targeting matters more than the message.

a plain message to the right people beats a clever one to the same list everyone else already bought.

## running it without a person

instead of paying a person to do all that, you run an agent stack. its a few specialised agents working in sequence:

a research agent reads the target market, the competitors, the positioning, and the actual language the industry speaks

a lead discovery agent pulls a starting sample of 30 to 50 ideal-fit accounts so youre never guessing on fit

a copywriting agent takes all of that, writes the messaging, runs it against spam tests so it actually lands in a primary inbox, and hands back a campaign thats ready to fire

what used to be a full week of an SDR "researching and personalizing," the agent does in an afternoon.

it doesnt get bored, doesnt quit, and doesnt need a 1:1.

## sending it and booking the call

the data and the copy mean nothing if the messages dont land or nobody books.

the send runs on owned infrastructure, not a shared tool that every other agency is also sitting on.

for reference: weve sent over 5,000 messages per account per month, for 18 months straight, with zero bans.

im not going to hand you the exact method here. what matters is that shared tools cant come near that volume without torching your deliverability.

then theres the booking.

an ai setter pitches a real time ("does 9am eastern tomorrow work?"), grabs the email, and drops the call straight on the calendar through the scheduling api.

no back and forth.

scrape, enrich, write, send, book. that entire line runs without you touching it.

## what it costs

its worth adding up what each way actually costs.

the rented version runs you a grand a month in tools, plus a $139k SDR, or an $8k-a-month agency on a 3 month lock. call it north of $25k just to find out whether outbound even works for you.

running it the other way, you dont pay a vendor for the data, one agent stack handles the research and writing and sending, and the booking is automated.

the next 1,000 leads barely cost you anything, and your time in it is about 30 minutes a week.

the rented version costs more every time you scale it, while this way the cost barely changes whether you send a thousand messages or fifty thousand.

## where it breaks down

two things before you try it.

it wont save a broken offer. if your offer doesnt land with cold traffic, more volume just gets you ignored faster and at scale. fix the offer first.

and on genuinely hard, high-ticket sales, a real human setter on the phone still closes better than any ai.

the agent fills the calendar, but a person still does better on the difficult ones, which is why we run both.

the first couple of weeks are mostly testing, not a packed calendar.

you get actual conversations and feedback quickly, then the meetings build up after that.

anyone promising you push-button money is lying to you.
