You're supposed to throw away ~75% of your cold email list:

• you start with 5k emails
• validate the emails (TWICE, no catch-alls)
• end up with ~2.9k valid emails
• research the prospects (use cheap ai search)
• score the leads: we do tier 1, 2, and 3
• remove the tier 3 leads
• end up with list of ~1,200 t1/t2 leads

The tier's are based on what signals they're putting out online.

E.g. If I'm offering a website builder, I want them to have:
• a shitty website
• growing staff count
• trying to hire... growth minded

Gemini or any research AI can figure out this information.

Now the offer: it's gotta be something so good they can't pass it up.

Easiest way is to give something away for free.

Not something like "free audit", I'm talking about something that solves a main problem in their business, completely for free.

You need to be picky and scrap the freeloaders.

If done correctly, solving one problem for free reveals another problem.

You charge to fix the second problem.

Once they're a free customer, it's MUCH easier to convert them to a paid customer.

p.s. this is why it's important to score your leads. You don't want to give free services to prospects with no budget.
