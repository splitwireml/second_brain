# Human Psychology-Driven Content: Framework Hierarchy

## Question
How do human-nature tensions, resonance modes, psychological mechanisms, content formats, and distribution choices fit together when targeting human psychology with content?

## Answer

The closest umbrella concept is [[human-nature-meta-skill]]. The most operational version is [[behavioral-science-ugc]] / [[ai-ugc-behavioral-biases]]. The combined model is:

> **Target a real human tension → interrupt attention → create recognition → intensify emotion → show a desired identity or future → provide proof → make action easy → make sharing socially meaningful.**

The prior list mixed abstraction levels. The clean hierarchy is:

```text
Audience pain → human tension → attention → resonance mode → mechanisms → format → distribution → proof/CTA
```

## 1. Human nature: the upstream strategic layer

[[human-nature-meta-skill]] treats understanding attention, desire, identity, trust, action, and payment as a durable meta-skill beneath AI, writing, coding, sales, and product-building.

| Human tension | What it targets | Content move |
|---|---|---|
| **Survival** | Safety, threat, pain, financial/social risk, failure | Name the problem and stakes. |
| **Identity** | Status, belonging, tribe, self-concept | Mirror who it is for and who it is not for. |
| **Progress** | Clarity, purpose, meaning, transformation | Show the next stage of the reader's story. |

The source argues that creators should meet people at their current tension: abstract meaning misses people still dealing with immediate safety or status problems.

Its five levers are: **name the threat**, **mirror the identity**, **exclude people**, **paint the transformation**, and **give the first step**. The final lever makes action small enough to begin; the Zeigarnik effect turns incompletion into forward tension.

## 2. Resonance modes: the message objective

[[proof-driven-content-rebuilding]] adds a different layer. A resonance mode describes the response the reader should leave with:

| Mode | Reader response | Natural human-tension pairing |
|---|---|---|
| **Understood** | “Someone finally described my experience.” Recognition and relief. | Identity; sometimes survival. |
| **Smart** | “I learned something useful.” Framework or explanatory value. | Progress. |
| **Warned** | “I may be making a costly mistake.” Stakes and personal relevance. | Survival. |
| **Access** | “I received insider information.” Privileged knowledge, numbers, or process. | Identity/status or progress. |

**They are not the same thing.** Human nature supplies the broad pressure; resonance mode supplies the immediate emotional/intellectual job of one piece. The table above is a likely synthesis, not an explicit one-to-one source mapping.

Resonance modes are usually **alternatives**: choose one primary mode and, at most, one secondary mode. Do not force every post to be understood, smart, warned, and access-oriented.

Examples: “You are not lazy; your system is badly designed” → Identity + Understood. “Your AI workflow fails because the verifier is missing” → Progress + Smart. “Every month you delay this, the cost compounds” → Survival + Warned. “Here is the exact workflow high-performing operators use” → Identity/status + Access.

## 3. Attention and mechanisms: how to produce the response

[[behavioral-science-ugc]] and [[ai-ugc-behavioral-biases]] are a mechanism library, not additional resonance modes. **Attention is the gate:** earn the stop and the next seconds before resonance, emotion, action, or sharing can work.

| Job | Mechanisms in the wiki |
|---|---|
| **Attention** | Novelty bias, orienting response, information gap theory, contrast, threat/physical danger, attractiveness/reproductive signals, resource/status numbers, social/conflict signals. |
| **Belonging** | In-group bias, exact persona language, pratfall effect, mere exposure, identity-signaling bias. |
| **Emotion** | Empathy gap, present-tense pain, loss aversion, FOMO, narrative transportation, awe. |
| **Action** | Anchoring, commitment/consistency, Zeigarnik/open loops, reciprocity, social proof cascade. |

[[scroll-stopping-effect]] frames scrolling as a motor habit that must first be interrupted. [[virality-mechanics]] adds four assessment variables: stimulus strength, neurochemical depth, resolution quality, and shareability architecture. [[meta-ads-neuroscience]] groups related paid-ad mechanisms as anticipation gaps, specific social proof, regret/loss framing, price anchoring, foot-in-the-door commitment, and follow-up consistency.

These mechanisms are **selectively composable**. One to three supporting mechanisms is usually a better working assumption than trying to deploy the entire library in every post.

## 4. Short-form implementation stack

[[behavioral-science-ugc]] gives this stage sequence:

1. **First frame:** novelty + orienting response + contrast.
2. **Hook:** information gap + FOMO/peer-group framing.
3. **Pain:** empathy gap + loss aversion + in-group language.
4. **Mechanism bridge:** Zeigarnik loop + anchoring.
5. **Transformation:** narrative transportation + awe.
6. **CTA:** reciprocity + commitment/consistency.
7. **Comments:** pinned social proof + identity signaling.

This is a campaign architecture and hypothesis menu, not a checklist of fifteen biases for one asset.

## 5. What combines and what is alternative?

| Layer | Question | Choices | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Target selection** | Who has the pain? | Niche, ICP, buyer language, use case | Upstream choice. |
| **Human tension** | What pressure is active? | Survival, identity, progress | Usually one primary. |
| **Resonance mode** | What should the reader feel? | Understood, smart, warned, access | Usually one primary. |
| **Mechanisms** | How will it happen? | Curiosity, loss, in-group, proof, reciprocity | Selectively composable; use 1–3. |
| **Format** | What carries the message? | Video, thread, carousel, landing page, long-form | Usually one primary format. |
| **Visual hook** | What interrupts attention? | Threat, status number, transformation, conflict, spectacle | Choose one or a small combination. |
| **Distribution** | Where will it travel? | X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, SEO | Channel-specific alternative. |
| **CTA** | What happens next? | Save, comment, DM, click, buy | Choose one clear action. |

The composable stack is:

```text
Human tension + primary resonance mode + 1–3 mechanisms + proof + format + distribution + CTA
```

The alternatives are choices at the same layer: resonance mode, content format, visual hook, distribution channel, and CTA. The prior “bias stack” should therefore be treated as a menu, not a giant recipe.

## 6. Existing content-execution frameworks

### Short-form, virality, and product launches

- [[4-beat-short-form-ad-structure]] is the execution unit: **Hook 0–2s → Problem 2–5s → Proof 5–12s → CTA 12–15s**. Without a proof moment, the source says an ad is content rather than a commercial.
- [[virality-mechanics]] covers threat, attractiveness, resource/status, and social/conflict stimuli; platform signals; resolution; shareability; and volume/iteration. Its platform variants emphasize TikTok three-second retention, Instagram saves, YouTube search, X specific evidence-based claims, and Snapchat raw authenticity.
- [[ai-video-virality-formats]] covers before/after transitions, emotional slideshow arcs, viral-copy-with-product twists, surreal physics, freeze-to-motion, self-insert loops, tactile transformations, lore/cellular spectacle, and ambiguity clips. The recurring pattern is familiarity → trust → engagement.
- [[mobile-app-organic-virality]] adds visual value proposition, a novel spin on a proven desire, curiosity/watch-time craft, copy-paste-adapt research, comment-to-DM loops, and conversion-weighted measurement.
- [[viral-launch-system]] adds research-first positioning, a bold claim that is novel, painful, and immediately understandable, customer voice, novelty extraction, hook critique, invention-novelty/copy-intensity scoring, the “Mom Test,” proof in seconds 5–30, aggressive filler cutting, and human ownership of the final 5%.
- [[viral-product-principles]] emphasizes simplicity, one idea per screen, visible pricing, emotional copy, customer language, outcomes over features, social proof, and one-step conversion.

### Brand, authority, and long-term trust

- [[brand-as-environment]] says brand is the worldview people absorb after roughly 3–6 months of consistent exposure, not merely a profile picture, bio, or color palette.
- [[personal-brand-establishment]] gives the equation:

```text
Personal Brand = Repeated Worldview × Visible Competence × Proof × Distribution Surface × Conversion Path
```

  Its content units include teardowns, before/after analysis, diagnoses, implementation walkthroughs, named examples, numbers/screenshots, reusable frameworks, and lessons from shipped work.
- [[x-organic-b2b-sales]] separates viral discovery from buyer conversion: hooks → long-form competence transfer → proof → profile/DM CTA. Its suggested mix is 30–40% discovery/personality, ~30% long-form buyer content, ~20% proof, and ~10% direct asks.
- [[linkedin-growth]] treats profile conversion, visual packaging, content, lead capture, DM follow-up, and outbound as one demand system. Comparison tables, funnels, pyramids, icebergs, and visual metaphors create fast comprehension and save value.
- [[content-os]] provides the production system: strategy, voice, sources, lifecycle, verification, scheduling, and feedback. Its bookmarkability rubric asks whether content saves a future task, includes proof, gives a reusable takeaway, targets a specific audience/job, works without the author present, and has a strong visual/artifact.

### Market language, product targeting, and distribution

- [[niche-specificity-digital-product]] narrows category → niche → niche-within-niche, lists ten specific problems and ten desired first-person outcomes, then selects the most painful/expensive problem.
- [[why-people-post-problems-in-threads-in-ai-age]] says public threads expose lived experience, validation needs, messy truth, exact pain language, failed attempts, objections, implementation gaps, and willingness to seek help.
- [[reddit-research-source-vs-twitter-distribution]] distinguishes Reddit as the research source from X/Twitter as the publishing/distribution channel.
- [[faceless-account-cold-start-distribution-problem]] shows that psychology-targeted content still needs targeted traffic. Its better sequence is **research → content test → audience signal → product → stronger distribution**, not product first and hope.
- [[distribution]] treats audience attention, comments, DMs, and repeated requests as demand discovery and offer-validation signals. It distinguishes audience ownership from distribution latency and keeps proof-driven rebuilding as a research loop.

### Character and production systems

- [[ai-influencer-path]] uses consistent AI characters, repeated short-form content, shares/saves, emotional identity, and multiple monetization streams; generic characters fail when they lack identity, personality, niche, style, and emotional hooks.
- [[mascot-driven-app-growth]] maps a persistent character's emotional states to product moments, gives it a product job, locks the visual reference, repeats it across scenarios, and measures installs/retention/revenue rather than views alone.
- [[faceless-content-system]] and [[ai-ugc-ad-scaling-system]] provide production-scale variants: generate many angles, create multiple assets, test hooks and creatives, review watch time/CTR/conversions, kill losers, and duplicate winners.

## 7. Proof-driven rebuilding and resonance selection

[[proof-driven-content-rebuilding]] is not another human-nature theory. It is a research/production loop:

1. Find older posts with demonstrated engagement.
2. Classify the original resonance: Understood, Smart, Warned, or Access.
3. Rebuild the underlying idea with a changed angle.
4. Preserve the resonance mechanism while changing the expression.
5. Match the CTA to the mode.

The five transformations are **niche transfer, inversion, specificity upgrade, before/after flip, and mechanism reveal**. Mode-matched CTAs are: Understood → a system that solves/prevents the situation; Smart → the full framework and examples; Warned → a risk checklist; Access → the complete breakdown or process.

## 8. Minimum working model and templates

```text
1. Identify the audience's real pain
2. Choose the dominant human tension
3. Earn attention, then choose one primary resonance mode
4. Select 1–3 mechanisms
5. Select format and distribution surface
6. Add concrete proof
7. Give one next action
```

**Understood:** exact lived experience → “you are not the only one” → explanation/relief → small next step. Natural combination: identity + in-group language + empathy + narrative.

**Smart:** counterintuitive claim → information gap → framework/mechanism → save/share CTA. Natural combination: progress + specificity + curiosity + bookmarkability.

**Warned:** hidden problem → concrete cost of ignoring it → proof/example → checklist or solution. Natural combination: survival + loss aversion + threat framing + proof.

**Access:** insider number/process → why most people do not see it → evidence → full breakdown, product, or DM. Natural combination: identity/status + specificity + authority/FOMO + proof.

The final distinction is:

> **Human nature tells you what underlying tension to address. Resonance mode tells you how the reader should feel. Biases explain how to produce that feeling. Format and distribution determine how to deliver it.**

## Evidence status

- **Confirmed as wiki content:** These frameworks are explicitly represented in preserved source material and distilled into the linked pages.
- **Likely useful:** This hierarchy is a practical diagnostic for choosing a message objective, selecting mechanisms, and avoiding over-engineered content.
- **Source claims, not independently established:** Reported views/revenue, universal platform thresholds, dopamine or mirror-neuron explanations, and numerical claims such as “loss aversion is 2–3× stronger.”
- **Practical caution:** Treat the bias stack as a selection menu and creative hypothesis set, not a guarantee of virality or conversion.

## Practical verdict

The wiki does not need another flat list of psychological tricks. The canonical architecture is:

```text
Target selection → human tension → attention → resonance mode → selective mechanisms → proof → format → distribution → CTA
```

[[human-nature-meta-skill]] is the upstream strategy. [[proof-driven-content-rebuilding]] selects the desired resonance. [[behavioral-science-ugc]] supplies mechanisms. [[4-beat-short-form-ad-structure]], [[x-organic-b2b-sales]], [[linkedin-growth]], and [[content-os]] provide execution and operating layers. Modes, formats, channels, and CTAs are choices at their respective layers—not one giant checklist.
