# Why People Still Post Problems in Threads in the AI Age

## Question

In today’s day and age, why are people still posting problems to threads rather than just asking AI?

## Answer

People post in threads because asking AI and asking humans satisfy different jobs.

AI gives fast private synthesis. Threads provide lived experience, emotional validation, social proof, multiple perspectives, and public participation.

## Why threads persist

- **Lived experience:** Humans share “what happened when I tried this,” not just clean advice.
- **Judgment support:** Beginners often cannot tell whether an AI answer is good or applicable.
- **Validation:** Many posts really ask “Am I crazy?” or “Is this normal?”
- **Sensitive context:** Money, clients, legal confusion, shame, or fear often need anonymous human witnesses.
- **Messy truth:** Threads reveal failure modes and exceptions that clean AI answers smooth over.
- **Question discovery:** People often know the pain before they know the correct prompt.
- **Multiple perspectives:** Disagreement exposes segments, objections, constraints, and edge cases.
- **Observable demand:** AI chats are private; forum complaints are visible market data.

## Why this matters for digital products

A thread is valuable not because AI cannot answer the question, but because it exposes:

- exact buyer language
- emotional stakes
- failed attempts
- repeated pain
- objections
- implementation gaps
- willingness to seek help publicly

## Practical verdict

Public problem threads are demand signals. They reveal the language and context that [[claude]] can later help package into products, but the demand signal itself comes from humans publicly trying to resolve pain.
