# Affiliate Marketing

A monetization model where creators earn commissions by promoting products or services through unique referral links. Common in content creator and UGC contexts.

## Source-described non-viral Instagram affiliate funnel (Pounds, 2026-08-05)

Pounds describes affiliate marketing through five Instagram AI-avatar accounts and a R.A.C.E. content sequence: **Relate** to a narrow painful problem, **Advise** with knowledge and a unique mechanism, **Captivate** by collecting a lead through a free guide, and **Enroll** by converting that owned distribution asset. The video is a curiosity/belief bridge to a comment and lead magnet, not the sale itself; the source places the harder conversion in email. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-built-a-500day-affiliate-system-on-instagram-2085058455597990043.md]

The offer example is Whoosh on Glitchy, where the source says peptide offers can pay on doctor appointments set rather than completed sales. It presents the appointment event as lower-friction and nearly CPA-like, while Amazon affiliate brands are said to need millions of views. These economics, the `$500-$1,000/day` outcome, and the claimed `3%` ready-now versus `97%` nurture split are source-described and unverified; payout, qualification, tracking, approval, and compliance details are absent. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-built-a-500day-affiliate-system-on-instagram-2085058455597990043.md]

The owned-audience handoff is a Manychat keyword comment funnel to a free guide requiring name and email, followed by targeted Claude-assisted email campaigns, including a source-described daily email sequence lasting six months. The source does not name an email provider, model/version, API, configuration, analytics, or deliverability controls. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-built-a-500day-affiliate-system-on-instagram-2085058455597990043.md]
