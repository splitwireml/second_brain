# Cold Email Campaign Plays

Nine source-described cold-email plays, their quick/medium/advanced ratings, audience and trigger filters, tool roles, paired copy frameworks and list-to-research handoffs. These are the course’s nine plays, not Adam Rahman’s separate twelve acquisition workflows. Source privacy checks remain specific to mobile-number and private-individual plays. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Michel Lieben’s October 6, 2026 course is preserved below as source material, not independently verified tool documentation, legal advice, pricing, platform policy or measured results. First-person statements belong to the author. Commands, prompts and templates are archival examples only: none were executed and no campaign was created. Only the source’s stated privacy, private-key, opt-out and approval constraints are retained. Linked destinations were not retrieved. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

## Source-preserved operational detail

# Step 3: Pick a play for each tier (days 6 and 7)

> Every play gives your prospect a real reason to reply. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

A play is a campaign built on one reason to reply, rated from ★ (quick win) to ★★★ (advanced). Start with a quick win. The full builds are in [Part 1](https://x.com/MichLieben/status/2102820264186970172) and [Part 2](https://x.com/MichLieben/status/2104609197707116605) of my GTM plays. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

## Quick wins (★)

1. Post Engagers · inbound-led outbound ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Every like on a post is a name worth chasing. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- The list: everyone who liked or commented on your company's LinkedIn posts, filtered to your ICP, minus your open deals in folk. GetLeads pulls them at $0.012 per engager (LinkedIn shows up to 3,000 likers per post). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Reason to reply: they already picked the topic in public. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Pair with: framework 11, Content Offer (Part 2, play 6). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

2. Campaign Ideas · value-based ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Three ideas written for that one company, before any ask. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- The list: tier 2 and 3 companies with a clear website. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Reason to reply: three campaign ideas about their business, each backed by a named customer's result. A free audit works as the offer too. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Pair with: framework 2, 3 Campaign Ideas (Part 1, play 10). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

3. Is This Your Number? · value-based ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

The subject line is their own mobile number. The email is the demo. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Who it's for: teams that sell data. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Where I saw it: FullEnrich cold emailed me with my mobile number as the subject line, "is this you?" in the body and free credits as the offer. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- The list: sales leaders whose mobile your tool finds (here, FullEnrich's phone waterfall). Delete rows with no mobile, because the number is the whole email. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Reason to reply: your product just proved itself on them. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Pair with: framework 1, AIDA, with the number as the attention (Part 1, play 11). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Check first: the data-protection rules where your prospects live (GDPR in the EU), since a mobile number is personal data. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

## Medium (★★)

4. Champion Job Changes · signal-based ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

People who already used your product, now at a new company. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- The list: past customer contacts and power users from folk, saved as Sales Navigator leads (a paid LinkedIn plan) with the "A lead started a position at a new company" notification on, or saved as contacts in Apollo and tracked with its "Job Change" filter (paid plans). Keep the new companies that fit your ICP. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Reason to reply: they already trust the product. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Pair with: framework 9, Champion Play (Part 1, play 1). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

5. Hiring Surge · signal-based ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Find the sales or marketing teams that grew fastest in 6 months. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- The list: Apollo's "Headcount growth" filter on Sales or Marketing over 6 months (paid plans), plus the open roles in tam_research.csv. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Reason to reply: new reps need pipeline from month one, and the open roles show more are coming. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Pair with: framework 12, Do the Maths, or 6, Team Size (Part 1, play 3). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

6. Lookalike Case Studies · cold outbound ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Every case study is a campaign for the companies that look like that client. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- The list: one case study with a hard number, then that client's lookalikes from PredictLeads' Similar Companies or Apollo's company lookalikes (a paid-plan beta, up to 5 seed companies), kept to the client's business model. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Reason to reply: a company like theirs already got the result. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Pair with: framework 6, Team Size, whose third beat is the lookalike win (Part 2, play 14). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

7. Replacing Who Left · recruiting ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Reach the manager before the job post exists. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Who it's for: recruiting agencies. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- The list: people in a role you recruit for who left a company you sell to. In Sales Navigator, set "Past company" to that company and "Changed jobs (in the last 90 days)". Then find their old manager, and check PredictLeads' Job Openings for the post. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Reason to reply: the manager feels the empty seat the day it opens. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Pair with: framework 3, Right Person?, asking whether they're hiring for that seat (Part 1, play 23). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

## Advanced (★★★)

8. ABM Orchestration · multichannel ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Ads warm the account, outbound books the meeting. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- The list: your tier 1 and 2 companies as a CSV with LinkedIn's company-list headers, uploaded in Campaign Manager under Plan → Audiences → Create audience → Matched Audience → Company / Contact → Company list. It needs 300+ rows and 300+ matched members before an ad set can run, and up to 48 hours to build (LinkedIn recommends 1,000+ companies). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Reason to reply: they've seen your name in their feed before the email arrives. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Pair with: framework 7, TIPS (Part 2, play 16). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

9. High Net Worth Individuals · niche ICP ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Every private jet has a registered owner, and the register is free. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Who it's for: high-end property and wealth services. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- The list: the FAA's Releasable Aircraft Database, a free nightly download with no login: owner names and mailing addresses for US-registered aircraft, with no phones or emails. Most jets sit in an LLC or with a trustee bank, so you find the person behind each one, and owners can ask the FAA to withhold their details. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Reason to reply: a handwritten note about something they care about, like property in their area, with no mention of the jet. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Pair with: a tier 1 email written by hand (Part 2, play 18). ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

- Check first: the privacy and calling rules where these private individuals live. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Pull the list for each play ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

1. Find the people in Apollo's people search, by job title, at the play's companies (two per company in tier 2), and export first name, last name, company domain and LinkedIn headline as a CSV. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

2. Find the emails: upload the CSV to Prospeo or FullEnrich and download it back with verified emails. FullEnrich's waterfall, which asks several data providers in turn, also finds mobiles for the accounts you'll call. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

3. Join the research: have Claude Code add each company's tam_research.csv columns, matched on company domain, and save the file under the play's name, like hiring_surge.csv. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]
