# Format Propagation

Format propagation is the reusable operating layer after identifying a conversion-capable short-form winner: preserve its viral mechanism, validate it across accounts/faces, and distribute variations without exhausting the format or concentrating the campaign in one account. [[mattgittleson]] separates finding a winner (taste) from multiplying it (operations) in his [[hardlaunch]] UGC guide. This page records his protocol, not a proven platform guarantee. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

## Copy → adapt → propagate

1. **Copy:** find an app in the niche doing numbers, take the format and post a version. Matt says he has seen apps reach $20k MRR doing only this, but it caps quickly.
2. **Adapt:** take the mechanism, not the script, and rebuild it around the product.
3. **Propagate:** run the winning version everywhere across accounts while it has life. The guide claims the same system works on one phone/account or a thousand; scale changes volume, not the playbook. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

A candidate must be viral right now, support 50 variations without going stale, survive a fatigue cycle (hit, dip, return), and carry the product visibly (full demo, or at least name/logo). A one-hit format is a lottery ticket; a format that can show neither name nor product is rejected. Format discovery and product-fit details are in [[mobile-app-organic-virality]] and [[ugc]]. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

## Stage 4: iterate the winner and operationalize it

- **Managerial control:** review all creators' drafts and send messages every single day; the author says granular oversight provides impetus and control over what ships.
- **Additive one-variable experiments:** change only hook text, opening line, visual wrapper, or ending twist in each test. Keep the underlying format intact so results remain interpretable, ‘like a proper A/B test’ in the source's phrasing. No randomization, sample-size threshold, test duration, or formal statistical method is supplied.
- **Mechanism diagnosis:** identify the central principle/formula driving virality and preserve it in every iteration.
- **Cross-creator validation:** one creator hitting proves a creator, not necessarily a format. A true winner repeats across different accounts and different faces; no number of replications is specified.
- **Do not flood every account simultaneously:** Matt says that can kill a format in a fortnight instead of allowing it to run three months, and makes all campaign views depend on one format. Those duration/algorithm claims remain source-described. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

## 50/25/25 VC-style format portfolio

| Allocation | Source-described job |
|---|---|
| 50% proven formats | Reliable view bases form the floor. |
| 25% iterations | Material changes to proven formats, each betting on one variable. |
| 25% moonshots | Genuinely new formats; most are expected to die, but they supply the replacement pipeline. |

The article warns that 100% proven-format output works until the format dies, after which there is no pipeline and the campaign collapses. These percentages are the author's operating prescription, not a measured optimal allocation. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

The separate concentration rule is account-level: work toward no single account driving most views. An account carrying 50% of a campaign is his single-point-of-failure example; one shadowban can end the month. Monitor account health continuously and rotate out underperforming creators. He supplies no automated health score, monitoring interface, allocation denominator, enforcement tool, or rotation threshold. See [[tiktok-account-infrastructure]] for his account-versus-content diagnostic. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

## Evidence and scale handoff

Matt reports a first adaptation above 60 million views, followed in the next 30 days by two additional individual videos above 20 million, nine above 5 million, and thirty-four above 1 million; the original reached 60M while follow-ups contributed over 100M. The article does not provide per-video data or establish whether threshold buckets overlap, so the counts are preserved as reported rather than summed into a fabricated unique-video total. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

His retained/delegated handoff is 20% upfront format/principles/direction, 75% delegated briefing/filming/editing/scheduling/day-to-day creator management, and 5% final taste pass. He says he owns both ends for every campaign; he delegates filming, editing and posting before strategy, prescribes formats and hooks, and rejects AI-written scripts/hooks. This is distinct from AI hook-generation stacks in [[ai-video-virality-formats]]; the source does not name any model, production tool, management software, scheduling API or brief format. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

He emphasizes boring repetition rather than replacement novelty: warm for a week, scroll an hour daily, make the twenty-sixth version of a video that worked in May, and have patience to make the same video 45 times. These are source illustrations of execution discipline, not mandatory video-count gates. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]

## Evidence-backed adaptation versus no-AI hooks: two attributed positions

Jaffa's **October 5, 2026** Claude + Virlo guide supplies a complementary **research** protocol: niche/real-person/organic gates; a **20-hook** bank grouped by **structure rather than topic** with counts and median views; formats only when **at least 2 different creators** use them, with **2 example videos**, content/filming/setting detail, and **top 25% versus bottom 25%** view-and-engagement comparison. It then asks Claude to adapt **5 hooks**, preserve structure, and write **20–30-second** talking-head scripts (no shot list), or branch one linked winner into **3 concepts** with the same hook/format. These are jaffa's prescriptions, not a tested extension of Matt's propagation/portfolio system. See [[content-research-agents]] and the verbatim [[content-research-agent-production-prompts]]. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-use-claude-for-content-marketing-so-well-it-2107130633029927004.md]

**Keep the disagreement explicit:** Matt's **October 6, 2026** guide rejects AI-written scripts/hooks, even after many human examples, and reserves founder taste at both ends of the 20/75/5 handoff. Jaffa's **October 5, 2026** guide explicitly uses Claude for evidence-backed hook adaptation and script writing, arguing the failure is poor inputs. Both preserve the winner's mechanism/structure, but they disagree about **who should write it**. Neither local source tests these positions against each other; jaffa's newer data-connected method does not erase Matt's no-AI rule, and Matt's later publication does not erase the Claude prompts. No connector or agent was created during ingestion. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-use-claude-for-content-marketing-so-well-it-2107130633029927004.md] ^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-went-from-filming-ugc-in-my-room-to-14b-views-in-2107530746923823444.md]
