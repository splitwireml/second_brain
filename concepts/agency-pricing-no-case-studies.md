## Definition

Strategy for new agency owners to charge $2k+ from day one despite having zero case studies — using methodology depth, personal authority, and money-back guarantees as substitutes for social proof.

## Why $2k+ Floor

- $500/month leaves no margin for delivery costs
- Low prices attract price-sensitive, high-friction clients
- Can't reinvest in growth on thin margins
- Can't deliver well in 2-4 hours/month (underdelivery → no case studies → stuck)

## Three Substitutes for Case Studies

1. **Specific Methodology** — granular technical description of your approach proves competence
2. **Personal Authority** — prior agency experience, certifications, self-validated results
3. **Money-Back Guarantee** — transfers risk from prospect to you; most powerful close tool

## Offer Structure

- **90-Day Sprint** instead of ongoing retainer (less commitment, higher framing)
- **Stack 8-12 deliverables** so perceived value >> price
- **Bonuses** at zero marginal cost (founder access, templates, office hours)

## Sales Call Framework

1. Deep discovery (2x longer than usual without case studies)
2. Methodology walkthrough applied to their situation
3. Offer reveal with guarantee prominently
4. Objection handling
5. Direct close — take deposit on the call

## Trade-off

Lower close rates than established agencies (10-15% vs 25-35%). Compensate with 2x activity. After 90 days: 3-5 clients, 1-2 producing results → first case studies → raise prices.
