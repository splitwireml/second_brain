## Definition

**Onboarding** is the process of guiding new users through their first experience with a product. Empty states are a critical part of onboarding — turning the moment when there's no data yet into an onboarding tool.

## Relationship to Empty States

An empty state is essentially the first experience after signup and is a key onboarding tool. The best software companies turn this moment into an onboarding tool, a product demo, and an emotional touchpoint simultaneously.

## Key Principles

1. **Clarity** — tell users exactly what to do next
2. **Simplicity** — one action, one click
3. **Show value** — demonstrate what the product looks like with data
4. **Set tone** — make users excited rather than confused
5. **Reduce anxiety** — assure users they can't mess up
