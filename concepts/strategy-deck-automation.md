# Strategy Deck Automation

## Overview

The use of AI (particularly Claude) to generate consulting-grade strategy presentations automatically, replacing work that previously required teams of junior consultants over weeks of engagement.

## The Claim

A 60-slide strategy deck costing $30,000 from a big consulting firm can be generated by Claude in 7 minutes using a structured prompt. The output matches or exceeds the consulting version in quality.

## The Automation Process

1. **Define role**: "You are a senior strategy consultant at a top-tier firm"
2. **Specify task**: Market context, competitive landscape, SWOT, 5 recommendations
3. **Provide context**: Your business data (industry, revenue, competitors, challenges)
4. **Specify output format**: Sections matching consulting deck structure (1,500-2,500 words)
5. **Add constraints**: Data-driven, specific recommendations, no clichés

## What You Save

| Metric | Traditional | AI-Assisted |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per deck | $30,000 | ~$0.17 (monthly pro rate) |
| Time | 6 weeks | 7 minutes |
| Iterations | Unlimited | 2-3 to lock prompt |

Typical mid-size business buys 2-4 decks/year: **$60K-$120K annual savings** once prompt is built.

## The 3 Categories Where AI Beats Consultants

1. **Strategy decks**: Framework pattern-matching + your context data
2. **Operational reviews**: Standard methodology + data analysis
3. **Market entry analyses**: Research compilation + structured recommendation
