## Overview

**Google Maps Data Extraction** refers to automated tools that scrape business information from Google Maps — including names, addresses, phone numbers, reviews, ratings, operating hours, and other business details. These tools are commonly used for lead generation, competitor research, and market analysis.

## Use Cases

- **B2B Lead Generation** — Building targeted prospect lists for cold outreach
- **Local Business Intelligence** — Competitive analysis of businesses in a geographic area
- **Review Monitoring** — Tracking sentiment across multiple locations
- **Operating Hours Analysis** — Identifying patterns in business availability
