## Source-preserved operational detail

## 4. PAS (as Soheil Saeidmehr uses it)

Skeleton: pain → agitate → solve → soft CTA ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Sample line: "Your energy bills keep climbing, even when your usage hasn't changed." ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Best for: a big pain they notice every month, backed by a strong case study. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

```plaintext
Subject: {{pain_in_two_words}}

{{pain}} at {{companyName}}, even after {{what_they_already_did}}.
Every month it stays that way costs {{what_it_costs}}.
{{your_product}} {{how_it_fixes_it}}. {{customer}} {{real_result}}.
Open to seeing how?
```
^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

## 5. Industry Challenge (by Patrick Trümpi)

Skeleton: peers' challenge → personalize → solution → CTA → PS ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Sample line: "Security officers at banks face losses of $9k per hour due to ransomware." ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Best for: an industry-wide problem with a named source for every number. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

```plaintext
Subject: {{industry}} and {{challenge}}

We work with {{role_plural}} at {{type_of_company}} like {{client_1}}, {{client_2}} and {{client_3}}, and most of them are dealing with {{challenge}}.
{{companyName}} {{specific_detail}}, so it's probably on your list too.
{{your_product}} {{how_it_helps}}, and {{client_1}} {{real_result}}.
Interested?

PS: {{something_personal_or_light}}
```
^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

## 6. Team Size (credited to Nick Abraham)

Skeleton: team size → bottleneck → lookalike win → result → PS ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Sample line: "Noticed your {{department}} team is around {{team_size}} people now." ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Best for: Hiring Surge and Lookalike Case Studies. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

```plaintext
Subject: {{department}} team

Noticed your {{department}} team is around {{team_size}} people now.
{{bottleneck}} usually starts to show at that size.
{{lookalike_customer}} hit the same point at {{their_size}}.
They {{real_result}} after {{what_they_changed}}.
Want to see how they did it?

PS: {{one_line_proof}}
```
^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

## 7. TIPS (by Aaron Reeves)

Skeleton: trigger → implication → pain → proof and solution ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Sample line: "Saw you're expanding to the US. At 3% FX fees, that could be $300,000." ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Best for: a trigger you can put a number on. In the sample, 3% of $10M in US sales is $300,000. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

```plaintext
Subject: {{trigger_in_two_words}}

Saw {{trigger}}.
{{fee_or_cost}} on that comes to about {{cost}}.
{{the_pain_that_follows}}.
{{customer}} {{real_result}} with {{your_product}}.
Worth a look?
```
