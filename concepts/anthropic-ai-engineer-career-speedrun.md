# Anthropic AI Engineer Career Speedrun

A 7-step framework for transitioning into an AI engineering role at [[anthropic]], published by [[h100envy]] in May 2026. The guide applies gaming "speedrun" methodology to career planning — using optimization strategies and AI tools to compress the traditional path.

## Core Framework

### The 7-Step Ladder

```
Step 7:    Offer
↑
Step 6:    Interview prep + applications
↑
Step 5:    Anthropic-specific knowledge
↑
Step 4:    Portfolio + open-source
↑
Step 3:    AI/ML core + LLM internals
↑
Step 2:    Production software engineering
↑
Step 1:    Math + CS foundation
```

### Key Numbers (2026)

- SWE median total comp: $710K/year
- Research Scientist median: $746K, top packages to $1.05M
- Timeline with 3+ years SWE experience: 18-30 months
- Timeline from scratch: 3-4 years

## Critical Insights

### AI Tools as Accelerators

The guide's central thesis: AI has changed the profession itself. Learning paths that took years in 2022 now compress dramatically with LLM-assisted study. However, the guide warns against over-reliance — Anthropic interviews test genuine understanding, not Cursor proficiency.

### The Values Round

Most technically strong candidates fail at the Values interview — a separate interview focused on ethical reasoning and alignment with Anthropic's mission. Preparation is essential and distinct from technical prep.

### 2026-Specific Additions

Step 5 (Anthropic-specific knowledge) is new since 2022 roadmaps. Includes:
- Constitutional AI paper
- Responsible Scaling Policy (ASL levels)
- Forming independent views on AI safety
