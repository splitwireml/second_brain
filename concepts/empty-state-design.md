## Definition

An **empty state** is what a user sees when there's no data yet — essentially the first experience after signup. The best software companies turn this moment into an onboarding tool, a product demo, and an emotional touchpoint simultaneously.

## What Good Empty States Do

1. **Tell users what to do next** — single clear action
2. **Make the action always one click away** — never two or more steps
3. **Show what the product looks like with data** — sample data or templates
4. **Set emotional tone** — make users excited rather than confused
5. **Reduce anxiety** — assure users they can't mess up

## Framework

Every empty state should answer three questions in 3 seconds:

- "What is this space for?" — short headline
- "What should I do first?" — one primary action button
- "What will it look like when I'm done?" — preview, template, or sample data

If all three aren't answered, users will bounce and many won't come back.
