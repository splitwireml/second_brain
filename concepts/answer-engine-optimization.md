## Definition

**Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** is a marketing term (alongside GEO / Generative Engine Optimization) describing the practice of optimizing content specifically to appear in AI-powered search experiences.

## Google's Position

Google explicitly states that "AEO" and "GEO" are not meaningful distinctions from Google's perspective. From the official guide: *"From Google Search's perspective, optimizing for generative AI search is optimizing for the search experience, and thus still SEO."*

The key implications:

- There is no separate "AEO algorithm" — AI Overviews and AI Mode use the same ranking and quality systems as regular Search
- No special file formats, markup, or content restructuring is required specifically for AI search
- The tactics that work (non-commodity content, technical foundation, people-first writing) are identical to good SEO

## Practical Verdict

AEO/GEO as terms reflect a real anxiety — that AI search requires entirely new strategies. Google's guidance pushes back: the content that wins in AI search is the content that would have won anyway. The differentiator is quality and uniqueness, not format or structure optimized for machines.

**Do**: Focus on [[generative-ai-search-optimization|the foundational practices]] that serve both traditional and AI search.

**Don't**: Chase AEO-specific tactics that Google explicitly calls ineffective (chunking, llms.txt, keyword stuffing, inauthentic mentions).
