# iOS App Lazymaxxers MRR Guide

A tactical playbook for building a profitable iOS app to $10K MRR with minimal effort, by Will (@athcanft).

## Core Framework

**Thesis:** Don't innovate — replicate what's already working, add a viral feature, and let TikTok ads do the rest. No UGC. No influencers. No organic. Just ads, a paywall, and a nap.

The strategy hinges on three pillars: (1) studying proven app patterns rather than inventing from scratch, (2) triggering a core human desire with a screen-recordable "aha moment," and (3) aggressive pricing with a retention offer at cancellation.

## Methodology

### 1. Pattern Recognition Over Innovation
Examine top-grossing apps in target niches — Cal AI ($3M+/mo), UMax (face rating), Pray Screen (faith/prayer lock) — to identify what emotional trigger they're hitting, how they structure onboarding, and where the paywall sits in the flow. The goal is to extract the underlying pattern, not clone the app.

### 2. Core Desire Targeting
The deepest-converting desires: attractiveness, health, wealth, sex, status, intelligence, identity, fear. Apps triggering "Am I attractive?" convert far better than productivity apps. The viral feature must be screen-recordable — a reveal moment that sells itself in a single TikTok frame.

### 3. Pricing Architecture
- **Hard paywall:** No trial, no freemium — pay to access the desire
- **Primary:** $12.99/week + $59.99/year
- **Retention offer:** $44.99/year presented only at cancellation (Apple-approved method)
- **LTV benchmark:** ~$30+/paying user

### 4. Ad Production Workflow
Before/after AI-generated ads using fal.ai/z-image + SwapTok to TikTok Studio. One person produces 10-12 variants in an hour. Native/rough aesthetic, hook in 2 seconds, comment bait to drive TikTok engagement.

### 5. TikTok Smart+ Execution
- $50/day starting budget, USA only, optimize for purchases not installs
- 2-3 days to test creative viability
- Scale winners (+30% every 3 days max)
- Target metrics: CPI < $2.00, CTR > 1.00%, CPA < LTV

### 6. Leverage Programs
- TikTok ad credit: matches first $6,000 spend on new accounts
- Apple's Small Business Program: 15% fee instead of 30%
- Cashflow planning: Apple payouts have 45-day delay

## Key Insight
**One winning ad can carry a business for months.** The entire game is finding that one creative and scaling it. Most founders fail not because the product is bad but because they don't isolate the winning ad fast enough and iterate on it.

## Research-led build, onboarding, and paid-scale variant

David Ch’s local article agrees with the existing proven-market premise but adds a distinct research-to-build workflow. It says to prioritize a consumer problem with existing willingness to pay; study multiple apps’ revenue scale, customer complaints, and acquisition channels; then target a 7-day (or genuinely necessary 14-day) first App Store release consisting only of onboarding, monetization, and the core loop. The source names Claude Code and Shipper as implementation accelerators, while rejecting a generic “build me a fitness app” prompt: download 5–10 leading apps, traverse their full customer experience, screenshot onboarding, paywall, home screen, core feature, results, and interesting interactions, reverse-engineer the decisions, then pass Shipper the plan, screenshots, visual references, user journey, main action, subsequent action, and intended feel.^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-manually-studied-30600-ios-apps-making-20k-100km-2100537116635672690.md]

Its conversion layer treats onboarding as a belief sequence—problem recognition → the app understands → it might help → desire for the result—followed by payment after a tangible taste of the product. Rather than presenting features, use questions for personalization/severity, proof, small commitments, and a value sample before the paywall. The source cites Cal AI's reported 123 A/B experiments, 160 paywall designs, 424 total variants, 61 onboarding-paywall experiments, >3× monthly-revenue growth, and 31% trial-to-paid improvement; these are source claims and do not establish a general causal benchmark.^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-manually-studied-30600-ios-apps-making-20k-100km-2100537116635672690.md]

The article separates paid amplification from paid discovery. Its hierarchy runs from low views through views, engagement, downloads, conversions, and low churn; after about 100 organic creatives, use the few that produced most downloads as paid starting points, varying hooks, creators, openings, and demos. An illustrative $1,000→500 installs→50 trials→20 paid-users scenario maps where creative, App Store conversion, onboarding, paywall, and retention improvements compound. It does not specify an ad platform, bidding configuration, attribution method, pricing architecture, or guaranteed unit economics.^[raw/articles/xarticle-i-manually-studied-30600-ios-apps-making-20k-100km-2100537116635672690.md]
