# AI Podcast-Style Ads

AI podcast-style ads are long-form, two-person UGC-style advertisements that make a product appear in a natural conversation rather than a direct pitch. The format borrows the visual language of podcast clips already common in short-form feeds and uses dialogue, skepticism, objections, and reaction to carry the persuasion. The reusable method is source-described by [[ceo-vlad]], not an independently validated performance framework. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-i-do-6mmonth-with-my-ecom-brand-using-ai-podca-2080778980555133219.md]

## Production pattern

1. Write a hook for the first three seconds, then have one character raise a problem and the other react.
2. Introduce the product as the conversational answer, explain the result, and handle an obvious objection aloud.
3. Use a skeptic/fix-finder dynamic so the skeptic asks questions a viewer may already have.
4. Generate the two-person scene in [[infinite-ugc]], extend it to the required length, and let the Nebula agent place B-roll and product shots around the dialogue.
5. Add captions and a soft CTA, then export from the same tool.

The source emphasizes natural filler words, interruptions, and reactions; overly clean dialogue can break the intended native-content illusion. [[claude]] is named as the scripting and hook-variation tool.

## Testing and scaling

The format belongs inside the volume-and-testing loop of [[ai-ugc-ad-scaling-system]]: produce 20 or more hook/script variants, upload batches to Meta and TikTok, read CTR before hold rate and ROAS, kill weak variants, and iterate on winners one variable at a time. The source's claims about $6M/month revenue, 30 ads per afternoon, and a sub-1% CTR kill rule are operator claims rather than verified benchmarks.
