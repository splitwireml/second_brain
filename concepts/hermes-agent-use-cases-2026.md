## The Real Shift

2024: AI answered questions.
2025: AI helped humans work.
2026: People started building AI systems that work instead of them.

The important shift isn't "AI got smarter" — it's that AI became persistent. The moment an AI system can remember, observe, trigger actions, schedule work, coordinate tools, and delegate tasks, it stops behaving like a chatbot. It becomes infrastructure.
