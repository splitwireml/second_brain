# Monetization

The mechanisms by which a product or service generates revenue. Core to the AI/creator economy — understanding which model fits the use case.

## Revenue Models in AI/ML Space

- **SaaS subscription**: Monthly/annual access to a tool (e.g., [[linear]], [[vercel]])
- **Usage-based**: Pay per token, API call, or compute (e.g., OpenAI, [[vllm]])
- **Services/agency**: Bill for human-delivered work augmented by AI
- **Marketplace**: Transaction fee on platform volume
- **Affiliate**: Revenue share on referred sales
- **Freemium**: Free tier with paid upgrades
