# Micro-SaaS

Small, niche software product that solves one specific painful problem, billed monthly, run by you.

## What It Is
- NOT a billion-dollar startup
- NOT VC-backed
- Just a $10-49/month tool that 200 people in a specific niche need
- 10 of those at $29/month = $58k/year while you sleep
- One that hits $20k MRR changes your life

## AI Made It Real
Things that used to take 3 months to build now take 3 days with Claude Code. The bottleneck is no longer building — it's finding something people will actually pay for.

## How to Do It
1. **Pick a niche you already understand** — solve a problem you personally have
2. **Ship in a week, not a month** — Claude Code, Stripe, Supabase, a domain. Ship ugly. The goal of v1 is to find out if anyone cares.
3. **Distribute on X** — indie SaaS lives or dies on the launch tweet

## Key Insight
"The build is the easy part. the distribution is the moat."

## Money Math
- Starter: $500-3k MRR if you ship and stay consistent
- Mid-tier: $5-20k MRR with a real audience and one product that hits
- Portfolio play: $50k+/month if you stack multiple products over time

## The Deal
Most people make zero. Most products die.

## Proof
- [[marc-lou]]: Broke in 2021, living in underground apartment in South Korea. By 2025: $1M+ in a single year. ShipFast (~$20k/mo), CodeFast (~$20k/mo), DataFast ($15.8k MRR), TrustMRR (~$35k/mo). 35 startups shipped, 5% hit rate.
