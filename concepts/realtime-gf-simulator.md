# Realtime GF Simulator

## Definition

A short local X bookmark describes a “realtime gf simulator” as a free, open-weight generative video system with controllable events. The export says it streams at 720p and 60 fps and is based on Wan, but does not preserve a product name, repository, or destination-page details.^[raw/articles/thread-aisearchio-2075684084815450306.md]

## Source claims

- Realtime streaming at 720p and 60 fps.
- Controllable events.
- Free and open weights.
- Based on Wan.

## Scope and uncertainty

This is a one-post announcement, so the capabilities above remain source-reported. The local export contains no additional technical description or evidence. Although the idea is adjacent to [[realtime-world-models]], the source does not explicitly call the system a world model; this page keeps the narrower simulator claim separate.
