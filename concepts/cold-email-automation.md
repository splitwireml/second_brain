# Step 5: Build the sequence and check every email (days 11 to 14)

> The opener does 90% of the work. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

## The 6 habits

1. Small lists > broad lists. In the 1M+ emails we went through in Instantly, 500 to 1,000 hyper-targeted prospects got 20-30% reply rates, against 2-3% on 100k blasts. Keep every list at 1,000 people or fewer, one play per list. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

2. Hyper-enriched > standard data. Tech stack, funding, job posts, case studies and the LinkedIn headline travel with every list. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

3. Personal openers > generic ones. "Saw you're hiring 3 AEs..." > "hope all's well at [Company]". ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

4. 3-step sequence > single email. E1 opener plus value, E2 a new angle, E3 the breakup, 3 to 5 days apart. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

5. Value-first > ask-first. "Want me to send a quick example?" > "Do you have 15 minutes?" ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

6. Fundamentals > fancy tactics. ICP: who exactly. Offer: what outcome. Copy: how you say it. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Pro tip: layer LinkedIn touches between your emails. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

## The sequence

Add three steps in Instantly, 4 days apart: ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

1. Email 1: the opener plus value, your framework email. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

2. Email 2: a new angle, with a second offer or proof point. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

3. Email 3: the breakup, three short lines: your last note, one last offer and a question about timing. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

Leave the subject empty on steps 2 and 3 to keep one thread, and turn on Stop sending emails on reply in Options. Between emails, tier 1 gets LinkedIn touches by hand and tier 2 through Expandi: a connection request with no note after email 1, a like or comment on their post after email 2. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

# The 9 copy rules, as a checklist

1. One message = one idea. Name the email's point in five words, and cut the rest. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

2. No copy saves a bad offer. The offer line alone has to earn the reply. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

3. Stack offers in your follow-ups. Emails 2 and 3 each bring a new offer, like a case study or an audit. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

4. Every line should be about them. One "We" or "I" sentence at most: the proof line. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

5. The goal is a reply. Nothing more. End on a question they can answer in one word, like "Worth a look?". ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

6. The opener does 90% of the work. The first line alone carries the reason to reply. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

7. Cut the fluff. They read on mobile. The email fits on one phone screen. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

8. Lead with the offer. The trigger gets one line at most, and the offer is the first thing the reader can say yes to. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]

9. Reuse your list. Reframe the angle. Next month, run the same list with a new play and framework. ^[raw/articles/xarticle-how-to-book-meetings-with-cold-email-in-2026-full--2107499997906514147.md]
