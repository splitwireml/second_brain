# Consulting Frameworks

## Overview

Standardized analytical structures used by strategy consulting firms to decompose business problems and generate recommendations. The article argues these frameworks are now accessible via AI, undermining the traditional value proposition of expensive consulting engagements.

## Core Frameworks Mentioned

### Porter's Five Forces
Analyzes industry competitive intensity through: supplier power, buyer power, competitive rivalry, threat of substitution, threat of new entry.

### MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive)
Problem-solving principle requiring solutions to be categorized without overlap (mutually exclusive) and with complete coverage (collectively exhaustive).

### BCG Growth-Share Matrix
Portfolio planning tool plotting products/business units on 2x2 matrix of market growth rate vs. relative market share. Categories: Stars, Cash Cows, Question Marks, Dogs.

## The Framework-as-Product Argument

The article contends that consulting is "80% pattern matching against frameworks the firm has run 500 times." The implication: these frameworks are now portable to AI systems, making the consultant's proprietary methodology less valuable.

The 20% that's actually unique—your specific business context—clients already have. They just need the framework applied to their data.
