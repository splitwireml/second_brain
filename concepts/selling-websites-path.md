# Selling Websites Path

Building websites for local businesses using Claude Code, selling via cold calling.

## The Truth
AI made the building 10x faster. It did NOT make the selling any easier. Building the website is the easy part. Finding a business owner who trusts you is the hard part.

## The Stack
1. **Claude Code** — the build engine
2. **Google Maps** — free lead source (businesses with bad/no websites)
3. **The phone** — to close (local business owners answer phone, don't read DMs)

## Pricing
- $500-3k per build for basic local business site
- $200-1k/month per client for hosting, maintenance, small updates
- 10 retainer clients at $500/month = $5k MRR

## The Three Ingredients (none of them are the website)
1. Google Maps for prospects
2. Cold calling to close
3. Social media to document the journey

## Who Wins
The people who don't quit after the 50th "no" before lunch.
