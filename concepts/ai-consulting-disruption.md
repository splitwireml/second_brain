# AI Consulting Disruption

## Overview

The phenomenon where AI language models (particularly Claude) can now produce consulting-grade deliverables—such as strategy decks, market analyses, and operational reviews—in minutes at near-zero marginal cost, while traditional consulting firms charge $30,000+ for similar work taking weeks.

## Key Claims

- Strategy decks that cost $30,000 from big consulting firms can now be generated by Claude in 7 minutes for ~$20/month subscription cost
- The work is 80% pattern matching against established frameworks (Porter's Five Forces, MECE, BCG growth-share matrix)
- Consulting firms reportedly already use Claude internally while charging clients the old rates
- By 2027, consulting deliverable costs will collapse as AI adoption spreads

## The 7-Minute Prompt Structure

1. **Role**: Define AI as senior strategy consultant
2. **Task**: Specific deliverable (strategy memo with market context, competitive landscape, SWOT, 5 recommendations)
3. **Context**: User's business data (industry, revenue, competitors, challenges)
4. **Output**: Structured format matching consulting deck flow (1,500-2,500 words)
5. **Constraints**: Data-driven, specific recommendations, no consulting clichés

## Cost Comparison

| Deliverable | Traditional Consulting | AI-Assisted |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy deck | $30,000 | ~$20/month |
| Time to deliver | 6 weeks | 7 minutes |
| Customization | Template + name | Your actual data |

## What AI Still Can't Replace

- Stakeholder management
- Boardroom presence
- Multi-month implementation oversight
- Relationship-based trust
