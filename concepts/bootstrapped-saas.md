# Bootstrapped SaaS

SaaS business built without external funding, retaining full ownership and profits. Characterized by lean operations, often one-person or small team, and creative distribution strategies.

## Key Characteristics

- **No investors**: Retain 80%+ profits vs typical 20% founder ownership post-Series A
- **One person operations**: Founder handles product, marketing, distribution
- **Creative distribution**: Leverage platforms (Reddit, TikTok, X) for traffic
- **Open-source as growth engine**: Free tier builds community, drives adoption

## Case Study: Postiz

Postiz reached $100k MRR as a one-person company with no funding:

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| MRR | $100k |
| Team size | 1 |
| Funding | None |
| Profits | 80%+ |

### Distribution Strategy

1. Open-source releases on Reddit (250k+ views per post)
2. Developer contributions and feedback loops
3. Multi-platform presence (Reddit, TikTok, X, Lemmy, Product Hunt)
4. Trend-following (N8N, MCPs, Agents)

### Product Strategy

- Reliability over features (scheduling must work flawlessly)
- Professional design (avoid "AI vibe-coded" look)
- Temporal for backend reliability
- AI agent integration for automated workflows
