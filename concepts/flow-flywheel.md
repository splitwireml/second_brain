## Definition

A weekly planning ritual from [[rian-doris]] that aims to prevent goal drift by reconnecting long-term goals to the next week's highest-leverage actions. The system centers a 90-minute weekly review plus a shorter daily review.

## Core Structure

The framework has three layers:

1. **Review goals** across long-term and short-term horizons so the current week is tied to a specific destination.
2. **Identify weekly dominoes** — the few actions with disproportionate causal leverage.
3. **Run a daily follow-through ritual** that turns those dominoes into calendar blocks and highly specific next actions.

## Source-Framed Mechanics

The article frames the system as a defense against **goal displacement**: the process becoming the goal while real progress stalls. Its operating metaphor is continuous course correction, like a plane repeatedly adjusting its heading rather than flying in a perfect straight line.

The specific planning stack in the source is:

- Purpose
- High Hard Goals
- Annual Goals
- Quarterly Goals
- Monthly Goals
- Weekly Goals
- Dominoes

## Practical Takeaway

The actionable claim is not "work harder" but "narrow the week to the few causally important actions and pre-allocate them before reactive work takes over." In that sense, the Flow Flywheel functions as an execution bridge between high-level intent and day-level behavior.
