# Agency

A services business model where a team delivers ongoing work to clients — distinct from product businesses or one-off consulting. Key distinction: an agency has repeatable processes and can scale headcount while maintaining quality.

## Agency vs Consulting

- **Consulting**: Individual expert, bill by hour, personal reputation
- **Agency**: Team with repeatable systems, bill by deliverable/retainer, brand reputation
